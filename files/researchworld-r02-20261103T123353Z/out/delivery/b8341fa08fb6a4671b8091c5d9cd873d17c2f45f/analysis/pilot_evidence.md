It is retained with this explicit limitation, not used as confirmatory evidence and not required to reproduce byte-for-byte with the revised code.

Final candidate integration increases points fourfold to 16,384. A conservative linear extrapolation of the immutable pilot is 2 seconds per K=3 trial, roughly 47 minutes for 1,400 K=3 trials, plus ~10 seconds for 500 K=10 trials. Allow up to two hours on a shared host; this is a planning forecast, not measured final cost. ## Numerical integration gate

`results/diagnostics_extended/integration_diagnostic.jsonl` compares all three joint score geometries at interior and boundary centers from five mechanisms, including zero-transform epsilons 1e-6, .001 and .01. For each case, 4 independently scrambled Sobol estimates are compared with an independent 2,097,152-point direct uniform-simplex integration. This yields 51 center/score/epsilon cases, each at four nested resolutions. The direct reference has its own finite numerical error; discrepancies are not exact mathematical-error bounds.

Across these cases, at 4,096 points the largest absolute mean discrepancy was .002921 normalized simplex-area units; at 16,384 it was .000856; at 65,536 it was .000115. At 16,384 the mean absolute discrepancy was .000089 and the largest scramble standard deviation .001583. For the most extreme zero sensitivity epsilon=1e-6, the largest mean discrepancy was .000320 at 16,384 points and .000071 at 65,536. Thus 16,384 is a reasonable final candidate for area effects materially above .001. Smaller distinctions require higher-precision checking or explicitly unresolved rankings; no universal numerical bound is claimed. Four-scramble 65,536-point recomputation of retained main contrasts/representative cases is planned as an integration sensitivity, separately from outer-trial uncertainty. K=10 efficiency is withheld rather than estimated with unresolved volume error.

Independent unit checks also compare geometric membership and volume at deliberately selected interior/near-boundary centers, including smoothed inverse-image sets. These checks caught and fixed a missing sqrt(K) Jacobian factor before the pilot. See `proofs.md`.

## Calibration variability gate

`results/diagnostics_extended/rank_diagnostic.json` and saved samples use 10,000 independently generated Uniform-score calibration samples with n=499, alpha=.1, k=450 and 2,000 new test counts. Measured mean true calibration-conditional coverage .9001285 versus theory .9; variance .00017523 versus .00017964. Empirical test-coverage variance .00022167 versus theory .00022455 after binomial test noise. Kolmogorov-Smirnov statistic versus Beta(450,50) is .007516 (p=.6217; a diagnostic, not proof). True coverage below .9 occurred in .4835 of calibration samples, illustrating why a marginal guarantee is not a guarantee for every realized calibration set. Exact proof and moment derivation are in `proofs.md`.
