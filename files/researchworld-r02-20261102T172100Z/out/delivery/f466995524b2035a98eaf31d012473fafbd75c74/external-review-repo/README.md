## Reading standard

The objective requires a complete research paper and reproducible experimental evidence on reliability of conformal methods for compositional outputs, including uncertainty and conditions of applicability. A review asks:

- Is the research question consequential and the contribution distinguished from the literature actually cited?
- Is the predicted object clear: an observed composition, a latent probability vector, or a class label? Is the coverage event defined, and do the conclusions concern that event?
- Are the assumptions behind any guarantee explicit, with its marginal, conditional, simultaneous, or other scope stated accurately? If theory is supplied, are the derivation and empirical check convincing?
- Do the experiments test the stated hypotheses with appropriate comparisons and controls? Are data generation or acquisition, fitting, calibration, evaluation, and randomness sufficiently specified to understand the evidence?
- Are reliability and efficiency measures defined and interpretable for the compositional domain? Are dependence, aggregation, repetitions, uncertainty intervals, and failure conditions handled consistently with the claims?
- Can readers check each major claim against a reported result? Are figures, tables, and numerical statements readable and mutually consistent?
- Can the delivered code and instructions reproduce the evidence, including environment versions, data versions/acquisition, seeds, commands, expected runtime, and figure/table provenance?
- Does the manuscript follow the selected venue's required official template, body page limit, and checklist, and include all required sections?
- Are limitations, unsuccessful runs, incomplete experiments, and boundaries of generalization disclosed honestly? Synthetic-only evidence and negative findings are acceptable when conclusions match their scope.

These are review questions, not a prescribed study design. A missing optional theory or a permitted synthetic-only design is not itself a defect. Disclosing a missing required evaluation does not supply its evidence.

## Scope and access

The external reviewer reads the published versions as delivered, not their production history or other positions' private code. It does not provide the internal release-approval review and does not author another position's deliverables. The prohibited prior study and any material discussing it will not be sought or read.
