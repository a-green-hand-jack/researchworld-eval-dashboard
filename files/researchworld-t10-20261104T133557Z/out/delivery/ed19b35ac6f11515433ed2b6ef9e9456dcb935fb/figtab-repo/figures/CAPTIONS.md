# Caption facts (for paper-repo; claim id in brackets). Numbers here are only descriptive of the plotted data; values are in the CSVs.

**fig_headroom_population [C1, C5]** — Asymptotic CI-width ratio (n labeled, N→∞) of the best control variate in each class to power-tuned PPI++ (affine in f), per real pool; 1 = parity, <1 narrower. Each class is fitted by 2-fold cross-fitting on the full pool, so ratios above 1 for flexible classes (e.g. GBM on forest, n_pool=1,596) reflect the estimation error of the adjuster fitted on half a pool, not a loss of the bound. Error bars: SD over 10 random 2-fold splits (split noise only; no sampling uncertainty of the pool). Grey band ±1%. r = corr(Y, f). Source `results/headroom_population.csv`, script `code/fig_headroom_population.py`. PPI (λ=1) is wider because f is badly scaled (gene expression) or biased in scale.

**fig_validity [C0]** — Empirical coverage of 95% intervals vs labeled size n (log axis), 7 pools; 2000 replicates per cell, labeled/unlabeled drawn i.i.d. with replacement from the pool, N = min(pool, 20 000), truth = pool mean. Error bars: Wilson 95% interval. Horizontal line: nominal 0.95. Wald PPI/PPI++ undercover for n ≲ 200 on skewed/rare-outcome pools (census income worst: 0.755 at n=15 for PPI++ Wald, 0.926 at n=500); the studentised bootstrap over-corrects at n=15 on forest and alphafold (0.66, 0.70). Source `results/summary_validity_real.csv`, script `code/fig_validity.py`.

**tab_datasets [context]** — real pools of ppi-python v0.2.3; skew and corr(Y,f) on the full pool.

**tab_headroom [C1, C5; appendix]** — Same numbers as fig_headroom_population (rho=0, N→∞), as mean±SD over 10 cross-fitting splits; "--" = class not applicable (no X).

**fig_c2_real [C2, C0]** — Mean estimation on four real pools. Top: paired width ratio of each cross-fitted adjuster (Student-t calibration) to PPI++ with the same t calibration (like-for-like, `ratio_lfl`; <1 narrower), geometric mean over replicates with 95% delta-method band; log axis. Bottom: coverage of the 95% interval (Wilson 95% bars); PPI++ with Wald, t and studentised-bootstrap calibration shown dashed as references. n = 50-1000, 2000 replicates per cell, labeled and unlabeled sets drawn i.i.d. with replacement from the pool, N = min(pool, 10000) (forest: 1,596), truth = pool mean. Plotted data: adjusters are wider than PPI++ at every n except forest at n=1000 (isotonic 0.997, GBM-f 0.995, i.e. <0.5% narrower); ratios 1.00-1.3, largest at n=50 and shrinking toward 1 as n grows, with one outlier (GBM on f, gene expression n=50: 2.06, at the top of the axis); in line with the population headroom ≤ 1-2% of fig_headroom_population. Source `summary_mean_real.csv`, `code/fig_c2_real.py`. Appendix `fig_c2_real_app_a`: alphafold, census_healthcare, census_education (n = 100, 500 only, 1000 replicates) and plankton without distribution shift.

**fig_c3_synth [C3]** — Synthetic knob family, E[Y|f] = cos φ·z + sin φ·(z²−1)/√2 with z the standardised prediction, φ = 0, 30, 60, 90° (φ=0: affine, no headroom beyond PPI++), rho = n/N = 0.1 (N = 10n), 2000 replicates per cell (named scenarios in the appendix: 1000), known truth. Solid: measured paired width ratio to Wald PPI++ (equal to the like-for-like ratio for Wald intervals) with 95% band; dashed horizontal lines, same colour: asymptotic prediction (`theory_synth.csv`, `asym_width_ratio_vs_ppi++`); grey: oracle E[Y|f,X]. Bottom: coverage with Wilson bars. Plotted data: headroom is realised only above a threshold n that grows with the flexibility of the adjuster (at φ=90°: cubic reaches ≈0.75 at n=1000, within the asymptote; GBM is 1.25 at n=50, 0.95 at n=100 and 0.78 at n=1000; the linear-in-f adjuster is wider than PPI++ since the relation is quadratic); the linear adjuster undercovers at φ=90° (≈0.91-0.93). Source `summary_synth.csv`, `code/fig_c3_synth.py`. Appendix: knob at rho=1 (N=n) and named scenarios at rho = 0.1 and 1, with the (f,X) adjusters; coverage axis starts at 0.84.

**tab_ols [C4]** — OLS of income on (age, sex), census_income, no intercept; coordinate-wise 95% intervals, 2000 replicates per cell, n = 50, 100, 200, 500, 1000. Left: paired width ratio to ppi_py PPI++ (<1 narrower); right: coverage (Monte Carlo SE ≈ 0.005). Plotted data: PPI and PPI++ coordinate-wise controls are at parity (1.000-1.002); lin.(f,X) is 2.8% narrower from n=500 for both coefficients but 2% wider at n=100; GBM(f,X) breaks even only at n≈1000; at n=50 lin.(f,X) is 6-7% and GBM(f,X) 17-18% wider than PPI++ (sign change of lin.(f,X) between n=100 and 200); all intervals undercover the age coefficient at n ≤ 500 (0.79-0.85 at n=50, 0.84-0.92 at n=100-200). Source `headline_ols_real.csv`, `code/tab_ols.py`.
