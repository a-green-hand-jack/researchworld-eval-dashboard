\textbf{Mean validity does not guarantee every calibrated run.} Empirical cumulative distributions of finite-test joint coverage across 100 independent datasets in the iid heterogeneous $K=3$ setting, for calibration sizes 99 and 499. Global, training-only scaled and observed-group Aitchison methods share datasets within each replication. Each plotted coverage uses 5000 independent test responses conditional on its fitted and calibrated predictor; it still contains finite-test noise. The dashed line marks 0.90. These distributions and their empirical lower tails are descriptive, not certified probabilities of population conditional validity.
