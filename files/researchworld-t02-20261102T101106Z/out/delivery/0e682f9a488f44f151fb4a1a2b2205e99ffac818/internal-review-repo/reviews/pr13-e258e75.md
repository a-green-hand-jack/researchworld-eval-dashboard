## Conclusion: changes requested (not approved)
The paper is honest and well scoped; figures are non-blank, labelled and match the data. The open points are listed below.

### F1 (substantive, analysis + report): A2(ii) "refuted" depends on the norm measure, and the alternative is already computed but not delivered
Paper: width coefficient in log D ~ log t_fit + log||theta_fit|| + log d is -0.57 [-1.17,-0.13] (66 runs) → "only through norm refuted", abstract "mechanism fails". The norm used is total L2 of all parameters, which grows with sqrt(#params) and so is nearly collinear with width (the paper says so). experiment-repo 7c3e2e3 (`results/mech_stats.json`, not in this PR) adds the per-parameter RMS-norm regression: all 66 runs, width coefficient +0.59 [-0.83, 2.26] (includes 0); main-only n=26: +3.68 [0.34, 12.6] with norm coefficient 5.3 [1.3, 16.8] (wild, collinear). So the refutation flips to inconclusive under a scale-free norm. Required: deliver 7c3e2e3's results through experiment-repo, report both norm measures in Sec 4.2/Table 2/abstract, and state A2(ii) as "inconclusive/measure-dependent" (or justify why total L2 is the right measure) — do not call the mechanism refuted on A2(ii) alone. A2(iii) "refuted at d=128, marginally" stands on its own but its CI upper end (-0.08) is inside the post-hoc band; the abstract should say "marginally".

### F2 (substantive, analysis): the learning-rate confound is the main threat to the headline and is under-tested
The headline (wider ⇒ sooner) is under fixed lr 1e-3 across widths. The only check is lrscale, n=3 at two widths: 3/3 vs 0/3 (fixed) → 1/3 vs 0/3 (scaled); both contrasts are non-significant (Fisher p=0.10 and 1.0), and the paper itself says the check cannot settle the question. experiment-repo already defined lrscale seeds 3–4 (7c3e2e3) but they are not run/delivered. A study whose question is the role of scale cannot leave "is it just effective learning rate?" open when the runs are cheap (≈1 h each). Required (implementation, not a limitation): run lrscale seeds 3–4 at d=32,128 (4 runs), preferably the lr∝1/√d grid over d=16…256 with ≥3 seeds, and report A1's slope under it with censoring; if not done, the report must say it was not done because of a stated resource reason, with the number of core-hours left.

### F3 (claim wording, report): abstract/limitations over-read
a) Abstract: "narrow widths 16 and 32 generalize late rather than never" — at 100k steps only 1/3 (d=16) and 2/3 (d=32) seeds generalized; 2/3 and 1/3 still did not. Say "some seeds generalize late (35–58k steps); others not by 100k".
b) Abstract: "predictions and decision rules were written down before the main analysis" — Amendment 2 and the negligibility bands were set after results; say "pre-specified and later amended (Sec. 3)" in the abstract.
c) d=8: "generalizes without a memorization phase" (Sec 4.1) while Table 1 classifies 0 'direct', 4 'neither', and final val acc is 0.56–0.97 (not ≥0.99, the paper's own definition of generalization). Reword as "reaches intermediate-to-high validation accuracy without fitting the training set to 99%".
d) Limitation (4) and Sec 4.2 closing: "the lr-rescaling check suggests the effect depends on [fixed lr]" / "C1 is at most one compatible reading" — with p=0.10/1.0 and n=3 the check does not suggest it; say "cannot exclude".
e) A1 is supported at the band edge for the 30k budget (upper CI end -0.1038 vs band -0.10) and is "inconclusive" for d≥32 only (-0.31 [-0.62,0.12]); the abstract gives only the favourable CI. Add the d≥32 interval or "marginal" to the abstract.
