Open findings (request changes):
1. Eq. (newton) and Appendix A claim the Newton step reaches w*_r exactly, yet Unlearned(+) (exact H_r) is 2-7 points below Retrained in Fig. 1 (CIFAR-100/Dogs/Caltech) and in Tab. 2. The paper never reconciles these; a referee will ask why the exact-Hessian update is not retraining. Say what in the experiments departs from the idealisation (non-quadratic/finite-optimisation training, linearisation, classification via quadratic loss, etc.) or state that the logs do not say, and qualify "exactly" to the idealised setting.
2. Sec. 3.3: the Lipschitz "justification" gives a first-order residual bound 2*Lambda*||delta||, which for small perturbations exceeds the second-order signal 0.5*delta^T H delta that the SDP is meant to identify; as written the bound does not support identifiability. State this honestly (the surrogate is a heuristic whose accuracy is judged empirically) instead of presenting it as a justification; also say that Eq. (surrogate)/(sdp) are our formulation of the idea and that solver and perturbation scale are not recorded in the logs.
3. Unexplained contrast: linear CIFAR-10 collapses at 15% forgetting (14-15 pts) while mixed-linear CIFAR-100 stays within ~1-2 points of Retrained in test accuracy at 15-20%. Discuss (different datasets/models, single runs) rather than leaving it for the reader.
4. Writing polish (minor): Sec. 4.1 "Reporting" and Sec. 4.2 contain meta-phrasing ("on our measurement of the bars", "our logs") that reads as a process note; recast as plain statements (e.g. "values read from Fig. 1, +-1 point"). Abstract's last sentence ("including a single-run evaluation, openly") likewise.
