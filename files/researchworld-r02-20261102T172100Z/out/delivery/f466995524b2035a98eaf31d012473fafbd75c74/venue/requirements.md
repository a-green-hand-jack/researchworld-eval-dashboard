# Venue and delivery requirements

## Decision and audience

Use **NeurIPS 2026 main-track submission format** for a complete research paper addressed to machine-learning researchers and judged against the main-track scientific standard. This choice implements frozen `deliverable.md`, item 1; the task does not request an actual external submission. The official 2026 Call for Papers links the 2026 formatting archive. The retrieved shell and style both identify 2026. This is the current officially linked NeurIPS edition verified on 2026-11-02; the handoff makes no claim about unpublished future instructions or conference acceptance.

Use `\documentclass{article}` and `\usepackage[main]{neurips_2026}` (the default option is equivalent). The official style must remain unmodified. Do not select `final`: the official instructions reserve it for accepted papers. The 9-content-page submission budget applies to this delivery. An agent is not an author; no human identities, affiliations, acceptance, disclosures or license grants may be invented. The anonymous submission setting is a suitable format for this research exercise. Conference administrative obligations (registration, reciprocal reviewing, attendance, actual OpenReview upload) are outside the requested delivery.

## Completion checks and limits of this handoff

The official files and requirements can be checked now. Scientific completion, runtime/storage use, bibliography consultation, reproducibility, license compliance, incident completeness and final checklist answers require evidence from the owning positions and **are not certified by this venue package**. An unmodified upstream checklist contains TODO macros intentionally; it is a template, never the completed submission checklist. The paper must include a separate completed copy, with no TODO answers or invented Yes/NA responses.

Before a version is approved, check the built PDF and text: main content ends by page 9; references start on a fresh page after the content to make the count unambiguous (local recommendation); all required sections and 16 completed answers are present; fonts and Letter size pass `pdffonts`/`pdfinfo`; no altered geometry or font compression; all declared files are nonempty; and all scientific/reproduction evidence in O01–O20 is assessed by its owners/reviewer. A nonempty artifact check alone proves none of that scientific compliance.

`venue-artifacts.json` declares the settled entrypoints paper.pdf, manuscript/main.tex, README.md, analysis/README.md and presentation/README.md, plus the venue-owned requirements, build guide, source manifest and three official files. These paths are covered by the merged CODEOWNERS. A full final manuscript must also include its completed checklist, bibliography, all included assets and all runnable code even where they are not separately listed in this nonempty-file declaration. Path ownership and the source package are separate from proof of complete results.
