Timing versus width (tf, add mod 53, train fraction 0.4, AdamW lr 0.001, wd 1, ceiling 30000 steps; n = 5 seeds per width). (a) t_gen over all runs; runs that had not generalised within the ceiling are right-censored (open triangles, plotted at the ceiling) and kept in the estimate. (b) t_fit and (c) D = t_gen − t_fit exist only for runs that reach train acc ≥ 0.99 (n = 1–5 per width), so the panels may contain fewer seeds than (a); runs that never fit are shown in the regime figure. t = first logged step (interval 250) at accuracy ≥ 0.99. Black: Kaplan–Meier median over seeds with 95% percentile-bootstrap CI (analyze.py); a bar to the top of the panel means the upper limit is undefined, an arrow that the median itself is undefined (more than half the runs censored). Text in (a): censoring-aware lognormal AFT slope of log t_gen on log2 width over widths ≥ w_min (the smallest width at which ≥ 80% of seeds fit the training set); shaded: widths below w_min, excluded from the slope. Diamonds in (a): the same seeds 0–2 at widths 16 and 32 re-run to a 100,000-step ceiling (dashed); the 30,000-step ceiling there is a lower bound on t_gen, not evidence of non-generalisation.
