The study uses public, de-identified tabular datasets (UCI German Credit and Pima, Give Me Some Credit, US Census folktables ACS for California 2018) under their stated terms; no new data on people were collected. The models are trained for measurement only, not for decisions about people. Reading single-run explanations as exact may mislead; we report where one run suffices so that the result is not used to dismiss adequate explanations either.
