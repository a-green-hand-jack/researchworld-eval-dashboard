\paragraph{Overview.} Every figure and table below is produced by \texttt{presentation/render.py} from the files listed in Table~\ref{tab:map}. Full cap maps, the cost tables for every cap and rule, and the replay and task-count figures follow; the target sets are the OLLB census (\num{ollb.ceil.mean.ntar} targets, score rules), the OLLB rank-stratified sample (136 targets, all rules) and HELM (\num{helm.ceil.mean.ntar} targets).
