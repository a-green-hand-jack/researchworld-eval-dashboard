# Completed robustness results

All scheduled robustness jobs succeeded. Strict-first repeats the full gain/ceiling grid. HELM changes 6 Borda, 28 median and 12 minimum budgets among cases reachable under both definitions; there is no co-only-reachable case. Open LLM changes 6 Borda budgets among jointly reachable cases and has one Borda co-only-reachable case; other rule budgets are unchanged. These totals span all five gain/ceiling settings, not just primary.

At primary unit-ceiling/full-gain settings, the raw-score Open LLM matrix and deterministic one-evaluation-per-model sensitivity have their own rows in `results/attacks_open_llm_raw.csv` (22,875 cases) and `attacks_open_llm_unique.csv` (22,480 cases). Do not pool these with the primary cohort. The primary raw-score medians for mean/median/geometric/minimum/Borda are 2/3/3/5/6, versus 2/3/3/3/6 for published normalized scores. The unique-model cohort retains medians 2/3/3/3/6. The minimum changes substantially under normalization even though the main mean–Borda contrast persists. Exact summaries and cohort sizes are generated in `results/summary.csv`. They change the score representation or rival population as well as the target cohort.

Task composition:

- 200 HELM task-bootstrap designs sample 57 columns with replacement, fixed seed 20261103 and fixed 79-model cohort. Across designs, median optimal counts range from 14 to19 (mean), 17 to28 (median), 13 to19 (geometric), 6 to25 (minimum) and 43 to49 (Borda). The central 95% empirical task-resampling ranges are 14–18, 17–27, 14–19, 8–22 and 45–48 respectively. These are sensitivity ranges under exchangeable-task resampling, not confidence intervals for true test scores. Correlated subjects limit a sampling interpretation.
- Six Open LLM leave-one-task-out designs retain the same 4,575 evaluations but aggregate five tasks. Median counts are always2 for mean, always3 for median, 2–3 for geometric, 2–4 for minimum and always5 for Borda. The negative paired median mean-minus-Borda difference persists in all designs (between -3 and -2), and across all HELM bootstrap designs (-31 to -25).
- The main separation between Borda and mean persists under these composition perturbations, while the minimum is much more composition-sensitive. This supports an empirical conditional comparison; no theorem of universal dominance follows.

Raw rows are `results/composition_{helm,open_llm}.csv` and pairwise summaries are `results/composition_pairs_{helm,open_llm}.csv`. Each row identifies the design/omitted task, cohort and task counts; the generator is `scripts/robustness.py`. Full observed matrices contain no repeated evaluation runs that would identify sampling variance of reported scores.

Monte Carlo uncertainty concerns the random-task-order baseline only. `scripts/diagnostics.py` supplies per-target Wilson intervals for the probability that a random subset succeeds at the optimal k, and conservative Hoeffding intervals for cohort-mean random stopping costs. Six-task random baselines are exhaustive and have no Monte Carlo error. No model-sampling confidence intervals are attached to the descriptive finite-cohort attack medians.
