## Denominators and computational cost

At k=4 the natural target set has 28 tasks, with k/N=4/32=0.125. The fixed-target check has six targets and 26 eligible query candidates. The budget-denominator CSV gives all tested k, including k=16 (half the original task set).

The requested algebraic costs are correct for this dense implementation: moments/PSD O(MN²+N³), greedy k-step selection O(kN²), coefficient setup O(k³+Nk²), one new model O(Nk) after setup, and dense working memory O(MN+N²).
