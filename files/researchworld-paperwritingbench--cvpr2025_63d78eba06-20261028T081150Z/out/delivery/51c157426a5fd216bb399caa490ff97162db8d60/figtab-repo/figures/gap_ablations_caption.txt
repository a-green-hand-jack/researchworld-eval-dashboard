Gap between Retrained and Unlearned($-$) on CIFAR-10 (absolute difference in percentage points; 0 is ideal) as a function of (a) the forget-set size, (b) the number of random perturbations used for Hessian estimation, and (c) the $\ell_2$ regularisation $\lambda$. Lines join single-run measurements from \cref{tab:forget_size,tab:ablations}; markers and line styles distinguish the four metrics. The gap grows with the forget fraction and shrinks with more perturbations; for $\lambda$ it shrinks for the three accuracies but not monotonically for the MIA score.
