## Design sufficient to proceed

The proposed design separates train/calibration/test observations, fits the clr ridge center and scale on training observations only, and uses the finite-sample rank quantile. Shared data across methods permit paired comparisons. Independent dataset replications capture variation from training, calibration and testing; a single fitted split with a large test set would miss calibration variation.

The candidate core has K={3,10}, ncal={99,499}, homogeneous/heterogeneous logistic-normal noise, alpha=.1, 100 fresh replications, and iid plus a known-group mixture-shift test environment. Seven methods include joint raw L2/L-infinity, clr L2, training-scaled clr, observed-group Mondrian, naive coordinatewise and Bonferroni coordinatewise controls. The naive control lacks a simultaneous nominal guarantee. Aitchison and orthonormal ilr norms define the same region and should not be counted as separate competitors.

This is enough to study marginal-versus-group coverage, geometry-dependent efficiency, calibration variation, and a precisely controlled shift. It is not evidence about all conformal methods, class-label prediction sets, recovery of latent probability vectors from individual labels, arbitrary compositional distributions, unknown subgroup robustness, or arbitrary covariate/concept shift. Fixed well-specified conditional clr regression and a fixed coefficient draw also limit model-misspecification and DGP generality. If resources remain, a focused misspecified predictor or alternative noise sensitivity is more informative than another coordinate basis implementing the same region; the plan owner decides its priority.

2. **Match coverage to the target.** Declare observed compositional Y and simultaneous membership in the full vector region. Classification APS/RAPS results have a different target. The paper must not imply that observing one categorical label identifies a latent probability vector.
3. **Use a common efficiency measure.** Uniform-simplex Euclidean area at K=3 permits cross-geometry comparisons but is an explicitly chosen reference measure, not a universal utility. 4. **Separate mean validity from reliability of one calibration.** Report pointwise independent-replication intervals and paired contrasts as planned. If claiming stable coverage across realized calibration sets, add distribution/tail diagnostics with a predeclared tolerance and finite-test-noise caveat. A CI containing .9 is not an equivalence test or certification. At R=100, continuous iid rank theory alone gives approximate 95% halfwidth .00585 (ncal99) or .00263 (ncal499) for the replication mean; these analytical planning values exclude finite-test noise and do not describe Mondrian's random group size. Calculations are in `tools/feasibility_planning.py` and `notes/planning_calculations.json`.
5. **Retain group counts and stress-test scope.** Small observed groups have coarser quantiles and potentially whole-simplex regions. Known-group mixture shift preserves within-group laws by construction; its success cannot establish general shift robustness. Learned scaling retains marginal conformal validity under exchangeability, not automatic conditional validity. The analytically known group-mixture decomposition provides a check of that particular stress test.
6. **Zeros change the question.** clr sets exclude simplex boundaries. Censoring and deterministic positive replacement T_epsilon yield coverage of T_epsilon(Y), not original structural-zero Y. The final report must state what is covered and quantify epsilon sensitivity; a zero-safe original-target claim requires another construction/evaluation. The positive core need not wait for that focused sensitivity, but the paper's boundary conclusions do.
7. **Freeze claims before confirmatory inference.** Pilot seeds must be excluded from confirmatory runs. Record seeds, matrix, alpha, DGP coefficients, failures, runtime, environment and scripts-to-figures mapping. Do not pool correlated cells as independent replicates, multiply method/environment rows into a fictitious sample size, or promote exploratory comparisons into familywise inference. Pointwise descriptive intervals are appropriate if labeled.

## Replication and area precision judgement

One hundred independent replications are adequate for descriptive mean coverage and several-percentage-point effects in this bounded study, not universal near-nominal certification. For the rare group, ncal99 yields about 20 calibration observations on average. Conditional on a representative fixed group count of 20, ideal continuous iid rank variability alone gives an approximate 95% mean halfwidth of .0123 over 100 replications; at group count 100 it is .0058. Actual random group counts, training, score choice and finite testing alter these values, so use achieved replication intervals. Global methods need not follow a groupwise Beta law. Rare-group test samples average about 1000 in the iid environment at ntest5000; this adds noise that more test points cannot remove from calibration variation. A 10% empirical failure fraction over 100 replications has binomial SE about .03 before finite-test classification error; tail proportions require intervals and cautious language. The protocol now asks for these caveats and proportion uncertainty. No automatic increase in all cells is needed; if a central comparison is unresolved, expand its fresh replication count or narrow the conclusion with a recorded reason.

The area design is inexpensive and unbiased conditional on the independently sampled X anchors and uniform candidates. At most, conditional candidate-MC SE for 32 x 4096 Bernoulli memberships is about .00138 per replication; this bound excludes anchor variation. The protocol correctly requires retaining separate MC precision, checking close contrasts with more candidates or withholding the ranking, and treating zero hits as unresolved. Actual paired replication uncertainty determines the claimed efficiency precision. The fixed favorable clr generator remains a substantive scope limitation, explicitly acknowledged in the protocol; it does not invalidate a within-DGP comparison but cannot establish broad superiority.
