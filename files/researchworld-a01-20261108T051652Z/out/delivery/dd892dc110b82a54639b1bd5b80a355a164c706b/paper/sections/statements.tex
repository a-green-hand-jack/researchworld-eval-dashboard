\subsection*{Reproducibility statement}
All numbers, figures and tables are produced by the code in the supplementary material from the public score tables pinned in Appendix~\ref{app:repro} (commit hashes and SHA-256 checksums, cleaning steps, seeds, commands, expected run times, and the script that produces each figure and table); the solvers are tested against brute force and the closed forms against the solver output on every target.
The full computation exceeds the compute estimate given with the task and takes several CPU-hours on two CPUs.
ICLR 2027 has no mandatory checklist; the official NeurIPS and ICML 2026 files could not be fetched, so no checklist of those venues is included (the AI use statement is required by ICLR, the reproducibility and ethics statements are recommended).

\subsection*{Ethics statement}
The study analyses published evaluation scores and trains no model. It quantifies how cheaply a leaderboard ranking can be moved under assumed caps, which could in principle inform strategic behaviour, but it relies on public data, ignores any particular developer's practices, and is intended to inform benchmark design and interpretation. The two score tables carry no declared licence; we redistribute derived costs and small processed score matrices only (Appendix~\ref{app:repro}).
