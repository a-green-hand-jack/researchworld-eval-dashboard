# Whole-study computation ledger

This ledger accounts for every known executed analysis phase, including preliminary/failed work, while distinguishing recorded CPU from missing timers. It is generated by scripts/computation_ledger.py into results/computation_ledger.{json,csv}. Input hashes and archived source completion/resource records are supplied. No new experiment or retroactive timing estimate was used.

The selected, disjoint process-clock intervals sum to **28206.958919seconds (7.835266CPU hours)**. This is a recorded subtotal, **not a complete whole-study CPU total**. Several setup/correction/validation/test/postprocessing intervals have no CPU clock; their unknown entries are not zero.

Main whole-process clocks include all nine main fits/preprocessing/references/stochastic calls/imports/writes. Their reference/stochastic/residual breakdown is included as a decomposition only, not extra work. The four preliminary pilot files are distinct actually executed stages: initial pilot, dimension-specific budget pilot, first German exact pilot and final float64 retiming. Their recorded inner model-fit/reference/call clocks total708.345553seconds; no projection, safety factor or reserve is a measurement.

The ACS preflight timer encloses its training/reference/exhaustive/six-budget checks, so its nested call records are not added again. Three distinct smoke completion records and the later fresh reproduction preflight use their whole-process clocks. The background pilot and full arm use phase timers after startup/data loading, without adding their internal reference/call clocks or resource costs again. Original ignored completion files are archived under results/computation_ledger/source_records so every cost used here remains inspectable in the delivered repository.

The JSON also preserves deduplicated, nonadditive resource-job observations by job ID. It records managed main-audit/aggregation/claim-ledger CPU46.903526seconds and managed background-audit/aggregation CPU2.964626seconds where separate process clocks were not recorded. These are sampled resource accounting, not exact phase timers. The archived final managed-resource counter reports28279.181287seconds (7.855328CPU hours) for all tracked jobs through that snapshot, including tracked pilots/loader checks/main/background/postprocessing. It overlaps the process-clock subtotal, excludes sandbox-only work and sometimes undershoots process clocks locally; adding it to the subtotal or presenting it as exact total study compute would be incorrect.

## Explicitly untimed executed work

- The unsuccessful exploratory TreeExplainer control: five failed diagnostic comparisons preserve wall times and vectors but no CPU clock. The first strict abort is an additional untimed attempt. It remains a distinct failed control, not part of direct-reference correctness evidence.
- Pre-float64 strict common-game failures and the unsuccessful output-only precision attempt, then the12successful final common-game comparisons: no retained CPU timers.
- Pandas dtype/category corrections, data acquisition/environment installation/font-cache/library warmup and untimed parts of early model preparation: no CPU timers. Earlier and final ACS loader checks have memory/wall observations, not process clocks; wall is never substituted for CPU.
- Analytic/metric tests, boundary/seed/provenance validation, repeated local progress audits, smoke aggregation, integrated-layout checks and local ledger/postprocessing work: no retained CPU clocks, except the separately listed managed postprocessors.
- Startup/data-loading/writing outside preliminary/background phase timers: no exact residual is inferred from sampled counters.

These omissions concern cost instrumentation, not missing scientific comparisons: all main/background reference/call panels are complete and audited. The supplied approximately24CPU-hour planning target is an estimate; the recorded process-clock subtotal and managed counter are both about7.8h, but absent clocks prevent an exact all-inclusive achieved-cost claim. The paper should report relevant preliminary/failed work and this instrumentation limit rather than relabeling7.49345main hours as the whole study.
