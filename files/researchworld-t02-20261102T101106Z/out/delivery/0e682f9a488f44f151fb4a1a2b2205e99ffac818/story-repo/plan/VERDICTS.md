## Not run / not done (must appear as such in the paper)
`splitfix` (split-seed vs init-seed noise separation), val=1.0 threshold sensitivity (stop rule 500 steps after the 0.99 crossing makes 1.0 only a bound; 0.9/0.95 thresholds are exact), p=113 anchor runs, second prime, alpha<0.5, finer f grid, depth/optimizer/batch-size variation, norm-matched comparison. `mul` has 2 widths × 3 seeds (6/6 grok).

## What the paper can claim in one paragraph
Under fixed lr/wd/init, one-layer transformers and MLPs on modular addition (p=53, f=0.4) generalize earlier as width grows (AFT slope −0.25 to −0.41 per doubling in log time, wider than seed noise), with the narrowest nets late rather than non-generalizing. The delay's inverse dependence on weight decay is consistent with a weight-decay-driven cleanup time scale. The proposed mediation by weight norm at fit is refuted by the width coefficient in the regression and by the direction of the init-scale intervention at w=128 (a norm-matched comparison could not be built); the init-scale effect is a total effect and its mechanism is untested. Whether the width effect survives lr rescaling is open (n=3).

## Negligible bands (set after the results were seen; round practical values, not tuned to the CIs; added at figtab-repo's request)
* A1 slope of log t_gen per log2 width: negligible if |slope| < 0.10 (< ~10% change in time per doubling). Supported if the CI lies wholly below −0.10; refuted if wholly above 0 or wholly inside ±0.10; else inconclusive. The 30k-budget CI [−0.38, −0.10] has its upper end at the band edge (−0.103): supported marginally; the 100k-budget CI [−0.57, −0.25] clearly.
* A2-i exponent b of log D on log wd: prediction b = −1 with tolerance |b + 1| ≤ 0.3. Supported if the CI lies wholly inside [−1.3, −0.7]; refuted if wholly outside; else inconclusive.
* A2-ii: norm-at-fit coefficient: predicted > 0, negligible |c| < 0.25; "only through norm" refuted if the width coefficient CI excludes 0 (Amendment 2 criterion ii).
* A2-iii init-scale effect on log t_gen vs the norm-at-fit direction: negligible |slope| < 0.10; refuted where norm rises with alpha and the CI lies wholly below 0 beyond the band, as at w=128 (upper end −0.08 is inside the band edge, i.e. refuted only marginally); not tested where norm is non-monotone (w=32).
* A3 grok fraction contrasts: Wilson CIs non-overlapping (pre-stated pattern 5/5 vs ≤1/5) = supported; otherwise inconclusive.
Verdict column in the tables should be computed from these bands by experiment-repo (analysis owner), not by hand.

## Correction (after experiment-repo#7: per-parameter RMS norm, strict band reading) — supersedes the A2 rows above
* **A2-ii** depends on the norm definition. Total L2 norm at fit: width coefficient −0.57 [−1.17, −0.13] ⇒ "only through norm" refuted, but total L2 grows with sqrt(#params) and so rests on a width-correlated measure. Per-parameter RMS norm at fit (`mech_stats.json`, `reg|...lognorm_rms`): norm coefficient 0.52 [0.13, 0.89], width coefficient 0.59 [−0.83, 2.26] ⇒ "only through norm" **inconclusive**. Final status: **inconclusive (refuted only under total-L2 norm)**; must not be stated as a flat refutation.
* **A2-iii** at w=128: strict band reading (hi = −0.077 is above −0.10) ⇒ **inconclusive**; the direction-only finding (CI wholly < 0, opposite to "larger norm ⇒ longer delay") is reported with its CI. w=32: not tested.
* **A2 as a whole**: the norm-mediation mechanism is **not supported** (no test supports it; two tests point against it, neither decisive). Wording "refuted" is not warranted.
* A3: with n=5 the pre-stated pattern 5/5 vs ≤1/5 cannot give non-overlapping Wilson CIs; f=0.4 w=128 vs w=32 is inconclusive (pattern flag true); supported: f=0.5 vs 0.25 at both widths, f=0.4 vs 0.25 at w=128; inconclusive: wd=0 vs wd=1.
* A1 verdicts per experiment-repo's `verdict` column stand (main and MLP supported; 30k upper end −0.1036; restricted to d≥32 at 100k inconclusive).
