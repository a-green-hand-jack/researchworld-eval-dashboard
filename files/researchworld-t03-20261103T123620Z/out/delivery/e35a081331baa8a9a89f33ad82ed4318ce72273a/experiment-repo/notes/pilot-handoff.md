## Concrete scope

See `configs/main-proposal.json` for machine-readable settings. Required German (20 raw features) and ACS Income (2018 1-year California, 10 raw features) plus optional OpenML37 Diabetes (8 features). Train-only preprocessing; 80/20 stratified split; fixed training cap 20,000 for ACS. Fixed logistic regression, 100-tree random forest and 100-iteration histogram gradient boosting. One-hot categorical encoding for logistic regression, train-only ordinal encoding for tree models; group columns by their raw feature. Positive targets: bad German credit, income above $50,000, tested-positive Diabetes. All functions explain positive-class probability, identity link. Missingness and ACS filter are in code/manifests. Prediction quality is diagnostic, not a claim that these untuned models optimize predictive accuracy.

The primary background has 32 uniformly sampled training rows; selected IDs and test IDs are recorded. Same sampled inputs/background across model families. Main: 60 uniformly sampled held-out inputs after excluding the first two pilot-only inputs, 30 independent seeds per input/estimator/budget; 97,200 stochastic calls in 9 fitted model cells. SeedSequence components uniquely identify dataset, model, case, estimator, budget and repetition. No training randomness is mixed into primary explainer variation.

Budget levels: German 128/512/2048; ACS 64/256/512; Diabetes 32/64/128 requested coalition samples. Kernel SHAP 0.50.0, `l1_reg=0`, identity link, explicit raw-feature groups; no default sparsity truncation. Antithetic permutation pairs choose an even number of paths within the approximate coalition ceiling and cache deterministic coalition values within each run. Counts include actual model rows, calls, endpoint work and background expansion. Nominal budgets are approximate work matching; compare actual row/time curves, not a claim of exactly equal nominal runtime. Initial high-budget exhaustive Kernel calls remain explicit deterministic controls, not stochastic repeatability evidence.

## Exact references are affordable

Full German enumeration on one pilot-only input per model evaluated all 1,048,576 coalitions / 33,554,432 prediction rows, streamed in at most 8,192 model rows per prediction batch. Measured CPU seconds: logistic 14.117862641; forest 150.929641592; histboost 166.029781025. Peak process RSS for that job was at most 381,500 KiB. Therefore the proposed main strategy is exact empirical-game references for **every** dataset/model/input, eliminating a sampling-error reference gate. These remain finite-background interventional targets, not population or causal truth. Verify efficiency and compare exact Kernel versus direct Shapley sum on independent small-game implementations.

The pilot projection totals 7.257900189 CPU hours for main training/reference/stochastic calls. A 1.5x safety factor plus measured timed pilot cost and a 2 CPU-hour setup/statistics/optional-background-sensitivity reserve totals 12.987927614 hours, below the supplied approximately 24 CPU-hour planning estimate. These are projections, not completed runtime. Exact German reference timing is based on one pilot input/model; monitor actual cost and preserve checkpoints. Dominant projected cells: German forest and histboost exact references. `pilot_cost_projection.csv` decomposes all 9 cells.

## Statistics and acceptance

Primary operational event: normalized L1 error <=.20 AND signed top3 overlap >=2/3. Denominator max(reference L1,.01 probability units). Show .10/.20/.40 cutoffs and strict signed-overlap=1 sensitivity; top1, unsigned overlap, sign, absolute error, tie-aware variants, rank, signal and boundary gap separately. Choices are declared tolerances, not universal utility/safety thresholds. Use pointwise 95% instance-cluster bootstrap intervals, preserving all seeds per instance; paired instance contrasts for estimator/budget differences. No individual 95% reliability certification follows from 30 runs. Reference exactness is for the fixed empirical game up to floating-point precision. Conditional inference excludes retraining, resampling of backgrounds and ACS population survey inference. No multiplicity-adjusted discovery claims are planned.

`run_study.py` validates source/config signatures before reuse, atomically checkpoints a complete input, records warnings/costs/attributions/references, and records a failure before stopping. Kernel constrained-design rank/condition and efficiency are checked; failed solves do not become successful vectors. `aggregate.py` refuses incomplete or ragged panels. A two-input/two-repeat development smoke run verified the entire writing/reading/aggregation path; those outputs stay under ignored `artifacts/` and are not main evidence.
