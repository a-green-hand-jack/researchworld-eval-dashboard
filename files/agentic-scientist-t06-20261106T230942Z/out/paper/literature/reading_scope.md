# Reading and access scope

References are selected by their relevance to the fixed-estimator study, not to fill a generic optimization bibliography. Direct identified works and bounded older-method queries were used; no citation expansion or broad ranking-attack search was used.

Original abstract text was consulted for all cited sources except Zermelo (1929), whose original scanned article was retrieved from Springer and the opening model/likelihood pages inspected. For Koh, Broderick, Chiang, Jiang, Negahban, and Maystre, original PDFs were also retrieved and abstract/introduction excerpts inspected. For Bubeck, the original abstract, front matter and contents were inspected. These are not claims of cover-to-cover reading. Remaining citations are limited to the general subject or method explicitly described in the original abstract text retained through OpenAlex/Crossref or the original arXiv abstract page. No theorem, numerical result, or novelty comparison is attributed to an uninspected section.

Bradley–Terry's original article was not retained as a citation because the available metadata lacked an abstract and attempted direct original-source access returned access errors. Hunter's consulted methodological abstract supports the model attribution instead. Ford's original record supplied a bibliographic string rather than substantive abstract text; it was not cited. Inaccessible or unsuitable records (including White, Kendall and an introductory bootstrap book) were replaced by relevant originals with readable abstract text. Unrelated automated retrievals were not cited.

Exact identifier lookup initially failed for one ranking-list DOI and one Huber proceedings DOI. The ranking-list DOI was corrected by a bounded pre-2009 exact-title Crossref lookup; the unavailable Huber proceedings entry was replaced by Huber's original robust-location article. An initially guessed arXiv identifier resolved to the unrelated 2015 article *SEISMIC: A Self-Exciting Point Process Model for Predicting Tweet Popularity*. Only its title metadata was inspected; it was not cited or used. Maystre and Grossglauser's identified older work was instead retrieved by a bounded pre-2017 exact-title OpenAlex query.

No known prohibited study, mirror, discussion, or citation-expansion page was encountered. Nothing was inferred about its identity. Failed endpoints and unrelated title-only records do not support manuscript claims. Bibliographic identifiers, retrieved abstract text, available original PDFs, and reading scope are retained for auditing. No literature-derived numeric result is presented as a measurement of this study.
