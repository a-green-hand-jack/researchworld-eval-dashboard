# Empirical handoff: full primary study and controls

The primary and robustness analyses are measured, not planned. Primary: 13 rankings, each with 500 random-edit replicates, 50 endpoint-only targeted replicates, 500 cluster bootstraps, exact attack refits, one-edit influence validation. All results are generated by `scripts/run_study.py`; summaries by `scripts/summarize.py`. Environment and arguments are in `results/primary/run.json`; individual `audit.json` files record fit diagnostics and elapsed seconds.

## Supported findings

1. **Found attacks differ substantially across these fixed rankings.** The initial leading-pair contrast reverses after 2/57,477 Arena edits (0.00348%), 46/3,355 MT human edits (1.3711%), and 61/2,400 GPT-4 edits (2.5417%). NBA-2006–2015 found counts are 1,5,5,2,2,1,2,3,4,8, corresponding to 0.0813–0.6504% of each season's games. Counts are successful heuristic attacks, never claimed minimum counts. Sources: `results/summary/primary/datasets.csv`, attack witnesses (when available) `results/witnesses/*`.

2. **The smallest edit counts coincide with unresolved baseline leading contrasts.** Arena's cluster-bootstrap initial leader–runner margin is 0.00813, percentile interval [-0.09410,0.10430], original leader loses in 45.4% of replicates. All ten NBA season leading-pair 95% intervals include zero. MT human interval [0.10956,0.64196]; GPT-4 [0.54993,1.11269], both excluding zero under this bootstrap. This is conditional descriptive uncertainty, not a simultaneous/post-selection confidence guarantee. Source: `results/summary/primary/uncertainty.csv` (raw replicates in each primary directory).

3. **Targeted leading-pair edits need not disrupt the full ranking.** At approximately 1% N, both targeted strategies reverse all ten NBA leading contrasts, while random reversal frequencies range 0–38.2%; for Arena the random frequency is 25.0%. Both MT leading pairs remain un-reversed at 1% for every tested strategy. Mean whole-ranking Kendall disagreement remains a separate quantity; the influence attack optimizes the selected contrast, not global Kendall distance. Random rates have exact binomial Monte Carlo intervals; those are distinct from the data bootstrap. Sources: summary `curves.csv`, `budget_k` and `actual_k_*` columns. Deterministic influence rows have no meaningful Monte Carlo interval.

4. **Arena's tiny found counts depend materially on modeling conventions.** Lambda .1/1/10 gives 1/2/11 found edits; restricting entrants to original incident support >=500 gives 2 edits. Removing all ties changes Arena's fitted initial leader and gives a 3-edit reversal for that new pair. MT primary versus lambda .1/10 changes no found counts; expert-only human has 41/2,668, and excluding 380 inconsistent GPT-4 ties gives 47/2,020. Source: `results/summary/robustness/datasets.csv`. Do not present “two edits” as a universal property of Arena or conceal the changed pair in decisive-only analysis.

## Interpretation limits

- Retrospective outcome reversals preserve the comparison schedule and all ties. They are not data deletion, new-vote injection, or an executable vote-rigging attack.
- Same fractional budget across datasets is not a causal design comparison: model sets, sample size, score gaps, coverage, tie rates, dependence and eras differ. MT matched-cell controls below address schedule and sample count, while differences in labels and score gaps remain.
- Fixed initial leading-pair reversal differs from the runner becoming the global winner; use `winner_changed` separately.
- Influence scores and searched prefixes produce upper bounds; there is no global optimality certificate or guarantee of robustness below a found count.
- Fractional ties are a BT convention; ridge regularization is necessary to specify. NBA static season strength omits home advantage and drift; date bootstrap does not cure this misspecification. Arena user clusters are unavailable. MT question bootstrap captures shared question dependence but not arbitrary crossed annotator effects.
- These snapshots support descriptive empirical contrasts, not a claim that one evaluation design is inherently vulnerable, nor a method-novelty claim.

## Matched-schedule and further controls

On 1,814 identical MT question–pair–turn cells, with one sampled human label per cell, all 100 human-label selection runs have smaller found top-two reversal fractions for humans than GPT-4. Mean human minus GPT-4 fraction is -0.01152 (-1.152 percentage points); this range describes human-annotation sampling on the fixed question set, not question uncertainty. Source: `results/summary/matched/paired_*`.

The 500-replicate joint question bootstrap reselects each resample's leading pair. Mean difference is -0.01243 (percentile range [-0.02278,-0.00110]); human fraction is lower in 98.2% of paired replicates, with zero censored searches. Human runner-up changes to GPT-3.5 in 106/500 replicates while GPT-4 remains leader; human leader also changes in 6/500. Therefore the follow-up control holds the original GPT-4–Claude-v1 reference pair fixed: mean human minus GPT-4 found attack fraction -0.01205, percentile range [-0.02244,-0.00110], human lower in 98.2%. Five human replicates have an already-reversed reference pair and are assigned zero additional edits. Sources: `results/summary/matched_bootstrap/*`, `results/summary/matched_fixedpair/*`. The latter interval is about **-2.24 to -0.11 percentage points**. These are heuristic-statistic bootstrap percentile ranges, not intervals for globally minimal attack budgets; no formal post-selection coverage is asserted. This supports a descriptive label-source contrast on shared support, not a general causal effect of LLM judging. Label ties and fitted gaps differ by source, and annotator/question dependence remains conditional on this collection.

Adjacent-rank diagnostics evaluate all adjacent original contrasts, with actual refits. Arena has 22/63 adjacent pairs reversed by a single edit; NBA has 15–22/29 per season. These are existence results. MT has no single-edit reversal among the five adjacent pairs under the found-prefix algorithm; absence from this heuristic is not a certificate that no single-edit reversal exists. Source: `results/diagnostics/adjacent_pairs.csv`. The sandwich gap standard errors are a diagnostic, not simultaneous confidence intervals.

Adding a fitted home-advantage nuisance coefficient to every NBA season leaves all original leading pairs and found edit counts unchanged; 300 date-cluster bootstrap intervals still all include zero. Source: `results/nba_home/audits.csv`. This addresses one sports misspecification, not season drift or causal sports-versus-LLM attribution.
