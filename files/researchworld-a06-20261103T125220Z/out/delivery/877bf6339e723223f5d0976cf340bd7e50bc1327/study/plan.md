## Questions and contribution

1. How far does a selected configuration's reused search score depart from fresh-question accuracy? Study dependence on question count n, frozen-bank size K, shared question errors, and real quality differences.
2. Can that discrepancy change a concrete superiority decision or uncertainty claim? Measure a prespecified decision with known truth in simulation and examine its real-data counterpart with audit uncertainty.
3. How do independent audit, split reporting, and simultaneous fixed-bank intervals compare with naive reuse in calibration, error, interval width, selected quality, and question/model-call cost?

The empirical contribution is a controlled, reproducible account of conditions, consequences and reporting tradeoffs, checked in one newly generated real LLM/MMLU-Pro setting. Selection optimism, independent evaluation and simultaneous protection are established ideas. No invention or exhaustive novelty claim is planned. A small or null real gap is a valid finding. Fixed-bank search is an automated tuning procedure; it does not establish behavior of adaptive prompt creation, arbitrary pipeline compilers, or larger models.

## Targets and data exposure

For a frozen configuration j and an explicitly declared question distribution P, let theta_j=E_P Y_j(X), with binary correctness Y_j. The selected identity is jhat(S)=argmax_j mean_S Y_j, with a frozen tie rule. Primary target: theta_jhat(S), the fresh-question accuracy of the selected configuration. Expected optimism averages mean_S Y_jhat minus theta_jhat over complete selection experiments. Coverage is marginal over selection experiments unless explicitly stated otherwise; independent-audit validity can additionally condition on S under iid sampling.

Expected procedure quality E_S theta_jhat(S), oracle quality max_j theta_j, and selection regret are distinct secondary targets. Splitting can select a different configuration with fewer selection questions; compare its actual target and quality rather than silently treating it as the full-search winner. Changing K changes the family, and heterogeneous synthetic cells also change the quality grid; isolate clean search-breadth effects in equal-quality cells.

There are three different evidence levels:

- Known-truth simulation: exact generating means permit bias, MSE, coverage and false-superiority measurement.
- Conditional real-cache experiment: iid with-replacement draws of whole question rows from a fully generated, declared cache have known empirical candidate means. Repeat the entire selection/reporting rule. Calibration here is conditional on that empirical distribution; it is not coverage for unknown future population accuracy.
- Disjoint real audit: conditional on the entire retained 256-item search reservoir S_all, the primary finite-benchmark target is theta_remaining(j;S_all) = sum_{x in B\S_all} Y_j(x) / 11776, where B is the pinned 12032-question benchmark. A uniform 512-item audit sampled without replacement from B\S_all estimates that remaining-benchmark accuracy for the selected configuration. All n/K resampling slices use the same complement of the entire reservoir, including when selection uses only n<256 of its items. The selected-minus-default contrast uses this same denominator and paired audit questions. This is neither the full-benchmark mean including S_all nor an assumed unknown-question superpopulation mean. A finite audit is noisy: a search-minus-audit gap, sign disagreement, or interval overlap is not known population bias, a population false claim, or population coverage.

Under the Tier C fallback, the retained audit is uniform from the retained-search complement after averaging over omitted original partition positions. Fixed-C conditional audit claims condition on retained search256 and its winner, averaging over omitted original search identities. Because runtime can correlate with correctness, the delivered analysis instead protects across B/C at per-design alpha=.025, yielding at least95% unconditional coverage after design choice; it claims neither conditional-on-trigger coverage nor unbiasedness. Experiment owns and has committed this correction before unblinding. Question-weighted micro accuracy on the remaining pinned benchmark is the primary real target. The primary gap is the observed search score minus theta_remaining of its winner, estimated by replacing theta_remaining with audit accuracy and carrying audit sampling uncertainty. For the full-search winner the reused score uses all 256 retained search items; conditional n/K repeats use their specified search subsets. Do not relabel either gap as an estimated gap to the selected configuration's full-12032-item accuracy. A superpopulation interpretation would require an additional explicit sampling/model assumption and is not the primary real claim. The actual loaded pinned MMLU-Pro counts, category distribution, item identifiers, and option counts must be recorded. Subject summaries are diagnostic and have explicit denominators; they do not redefine the target or remove difficult items. Category-balanced results, if added, must state different weights.

The draft spec's original smaller repeat count was superseded by measured pilot sizing; retain the run manifest as the actual specification.

Compare naive 95% Clopper–Pearson (CP), Bonferroni simultaneous CP, independent same-size audit, equal-total-question split, and equal-total-model-call split. Label the extra calls for an additional audit. Record point bias/MSE, coverage and width, selected true quality/regret, question counts and calls, and Monte Carlo standard errors. Exact null references and distribution validation support the analysis; do not rerun or expand completed research without a substantive need.

Decision: declare the selected candidate superior to frozen default candidate 0 when its lower marginal interval endpoint exceeds the default upper endpoint. Preserve the analysis implementation and alpha allocation. The error endpoint is the unconditional frequency of claims with selected true quality <= default quality. This is a particular conservative comparison, not a usual paired test or claim-conditional false discovery rate. Show the setting of any reported extremum and Monte Carlo uncertainty; zero observed errors do not prove zero risk.

## Required real LLM experiment

Newly generate and retain outputs from one pinned open instruction model on the supplied pinned MMLU-Pro revision `b189ec765aa7ed75c8acfea42df31fdae71f97be`. A CPU-compatible 0.5B or 1.5B Qwen2.5 instruction model is allowed; the supplied 7–8B size is a resource example. Model/backend/quantization hashes and actual available hardware must be reported. The experiment uses a frozen direct-answer prompt bank, greedy decoding and a frozen parser; invalid outputs are counted wrong and reported, an explicit difference from the original MMLU-Pro fallback protocol.

Freeze before main outcome inspection: model, full prompt bank and hash, decoding/parser/context limit, candidate order and default, pool partition and sampling weights, seeds/tie rule, n/K slices, reporting methods, decision rule, uncertainty calculations and audit-use policy. Main generation inputs may contain question/options only, never gold answer/index/rationale. Cached scoring records may retain gold for reproducibility, but those fields do not enter rendering or selection beyond the designated search labels.

Use disjoint search and audit pools sampled uniformly without replacement from the test items: the retained search reservoir has 256 items and the audit has 512 from the remaining 11776. For a fixed design, exact remaining-benchmark audit and paired-contrast intervals condition on the reservoir and winner. The delivered runtime-selected interval uses the additional two-design protection below and has an unconditional guarantee. The exact simultaneous search interval instead inverts full-benchmark hypergeometric counts, then subtracts observed search successes and rescales to the remaining-benchmark target; its selection-valid guarantee is unconditional over search-reservoir draws, not conditional on a particular reservoir. Naive/iid binomial search intervals are labelled comparators, not exact finite-design intervals. Validation questions serve backend/parser/length/timing checks only. Sample the candidate order independent of outcomes, retain candidate 0 as the default, and use its frozen prefixes for K comparisons. No outcome-guided replacement of candidates or favorable stopping. Do not use audit outcomes to revise model, parser, prompts, selected identity, budgets or analyses; any exploratory analysis after unblinding is labelled exploratory.

Real deliverables:

1. A frozen primary full-search winner and its reused score, simultaneous interval, disjoint-audit accuracy, explicit selected-minus-default comparison with paired question uncertainty, and search-minus-audit gap with uncertainty. Finite-benchmark sampling and superpopulation approximations must be distinguished; iid CP validity must not be asserted as exact for without-replacement finite sampling.
2. Prespecified n/K repeated selection comparisons using the same generated matrix, with rows kept paired across candidates. Present these as conditional resampling of one model/cache, not independent model runs. Report Monte Carlo error separately from uncertainty due to the finite question pools. Outer resampling of both pools must preserve candidate pairing and repeat selection where the target requires it.
3. Known-empirical-truth calibration by whole-row iid with-replacement draws from a declared frozen real response cache: repeat selection and compare naive versus simultaneous and independent/split reporting against that cache's exact means. This tests reporting on actual model correctness patterns while naming the finite empirical target. It does not convert the external audit into population truth.
4. At least one concrete real counterpart of the synthetic decision and reporting comparison. Show the decision under reused and audit reporting, paired effect uncertainty, widths and calls. If population superiority is unresolved, say so. Do not call noisy audit disagreement a measured false-positive rate. Where compatible, retain the same conservative rule for comparability; an additional paired analysis has a distinct label.
5. Raw outputs, correctness matrix, question/partition identifiers, invalid/out-of-option/max-token/context diagnostics, category denominators, and an exposure/compute/failure ledger sufficient to audit all of the above. Never silently truncate or exclude long/invalid items.

Real-population interval calibration cannot be directly observed from one noisy audit. Known-truth synthetic and explicitly conditional real-cache calibration provide that endpoint; the external audit provides fresh-item checking and uncertainty. This division is part of the inference claim, not an omitted evaluation.
