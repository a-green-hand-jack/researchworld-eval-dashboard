\section{Reproducibility, compute and provenance}\label{app:repro}
All numbers in the paper are read from \texttt{results/tables/*.csv}, which the scripts in \texttt{code/} regenerate from the raw replications in \texttt{results/raw/} (gzip CSV; every file records the configuration, the seeds and the revision of the code that made it). \texttt{results/CLAIMS\_MAP.md} maps each claim to its file, number and uncertainty; \texttt{figures/MAP.md} maps each figure and table to its generator and input files. The step-by-step commands are in \texttt{REPRODUCE.md}; we summarise them here.

\paragraph{Environment and data.}
Python 3.11.2, NumPy 2.4.6, SciPy 1.17.1, pandas 3.0.6, PyTorch 2.14.1 (CPU; used for the Dirichlet-regression likelihood and the CIFAR-10 CNN); no GPU. Synthetic data are generated by \texttt{code/cpsimplex/dgp.py} with fixed population coefficients (\texttt{coef\_seed=12345}). CIFAR-10 \citep{krizhevsky2009learning} was obtained as the parquet copy of the Hugging Face dataset \texttt{uoft-cs/cifar10} (50\,000 training and 10\,000 test images; md5 of the files used: train \texttt{7170dcda\ldots}, test \texttt{a52165db\ldots}); the official archive (md5 \texttt{c58f3010\ldots}) was not used because the download stalled, and we did not check the two copies against each other by pixel checksum. The creators state no licence. The CNN is a small convolutional network trained for 15 epochs on 40\,000 training images; the logits of the 20\,000 held-out images are committed (\texttt{results/cifar\_logits.npz}), so the conformal experiments do not depend on retraining.

\paragraph{Seeds.}
Replication $i$ of an experiment uses seed $\text{seed}_0+i$ with $\text{seed}_0=1000$ (A1), $50000$ (A2), $7000$ (A4), $9000$ (A5) and $0$ (B); the B-synth pool of replication $i$ uses seed $10000+i$; the ground-truth grid uses seed $777$.

\paragraph{Compute.}
CPU only, 2 CPUs per job, on a shared host that was heavily loaded, so the wall times below are upper bounds: one A2 file 15--40 min (45 files); one A1 configuration 0.5--6 h (volume computation dominates; $K=10$ slowest); A3 half-split 0.5--2 h per configuration (8); A4 about 1 h; B-synth about 1 h; CNN training about 4 h; B-cifar about 5 min per configuration; analysis scripts a few minutes each (\texttt{analyze\_a3.py} about 10 min).

\paragraph{Discarded and failed runs.}
A first A1 grid used 30 evaluation points per slab and was discarded because the ground-truth conditional coverage was too noisy; it was rebuilt with 300 points and 30 draws per slab. A first B-synth run estimated size-stratified coverage without a minimum cell size (errors of $0.1$--$0.2$ from tiny cells) and was discarded; the rerun applies the minimum-30-point rule. The first A2 files did not record $n_{\mathrm{te}}$, $\alpha$ or the code revision and were replaced by a rerun with explicit settings. A first DGP with extreme means (coefficient scale $0.8$) had an oracle region with zero Monte-Carlo volume and was replaced by scale $0.4$. Jobs of the half-split study were reported as failed by the scheduler because the launcher script was edited while they ran; their output files are complete (1000 replications each). No number from a discarded run is used. Not done: real compositional data, volumes for $K=10$, misspecifications other than the one quadratic mean, and jackknife+ at $n=200$.

\paragraph{Literature restriction.}
The task excludes one published paper on its own question, and any page citing or discussing it. During a broad literature search for conformal prediction with multivariate and Dirichlet-related topics on 2026-11-04, a search listing appeared whose title is a conformal-prediction paper on compositional data with Dirichlet regression; only its title and about 600 characters of its abstract snippet were seen, the page was not opened, and nothing from it was used. We cannot tell whether it is the excluded paper or a paper discussing it, and treat it as excluded; we did not search for it or for its references. Later searches by the authors on adaptive prediction sets, conditional-coverage limits and multivariate-response conformal prediction on the same day surfaced nothing on compositional or Dirichlet conformal prediction. The related work and its statements of novelty are therefore relative to the sources we consulted.
