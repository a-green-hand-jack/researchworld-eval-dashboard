\section*{Ethics statement}
The study uses only public aggregate benchmark scores; no model was trained or evaluated and no human subjects or personal data are involved. The analysis shows how cheaply some aggregation rules can be gamed in a stylised score-space model; we see it as input for benchmark designers and not as a recipe, since it makes no statement about real training cost and cannot identify any actual developer's behaviour.
