\section*{Impact Statement}
This paper presents work whose goal is to advance the field of Machine Learning, specifically the cost-efficient and reliable evaluation of language models. Cheaper evaluation lowers the compute and energy cost of model development, but unreliable shortcut evaluation could also give a false sense of a model's capability; our results quantify when such shortcuts fail (notably for models stronger than those seen before), and we recommend unbiased, uncertainty-aware estimators in that regime. We see no further societal consequences that must be specifically highlighted.
