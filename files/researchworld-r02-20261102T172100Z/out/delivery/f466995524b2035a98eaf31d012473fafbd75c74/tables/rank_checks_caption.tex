\textbf{Finite ranks and ties.} Each row uses 20,000 independent trials. Rank is $\lceil(n+1)(1-\alpha)\rceil$; $\infty$ denotes the whole-domain fallback. Theory is exact expected coverage including conservative Bernoulli ties. Test intervals are 95\% Wilson; calibration coverage (CDF at the threshold) has a pointwise 95\% $t$ interval for its trial mean. Variance checks and seeds remain in \texttt{tables/rank\_checks.csv}.
