\section{Reproduction: commands and measured times}
\label{app:repro}
From \texttt{analysis/}, with the pinned \texttt{requirements.lock} (Python 3.11.2) and GNU make 4.4.1: \texttt{make data} (re-download and verify the public inputs against the SHA-256 values above), \texttt{make all} (tests, all frames, caps, sensitivity and control panels, validation, summaries; \texttt{make quick} is only a partial run of the HELM rules and the Open LLM Leaderboard score rules), and, for the review and final additions alone, \texttt{make review-checks tables} and \texttt{make final-additions}. A fresh isolated export is recomputed and compared row by row (absolute tolerance $10^{-5}$, infinity and NaN statuses matched) with \texttt{src/clean\_reproduce.py}, \texttt{src/clean\_review\_reproduce.py} and \texttt{src/clean\_final\_reproduce.py}. Table~\ref{tab:times} lists the measured times on two CPUs. The original 16-file study (the seven main and sensitivity files and the nine robustness and control files; 457{,}772 rows) was recomputed from an initially empty export (\texttt{src/clean\_reproduce.py}) and compared at absolute tolerance $10^{-5}$ with matching infinity and NaN statuses: it passed, with a maximum finite difference of $5.8\times10^{-9}$ (HELM main) and zero difference in all 174{,}000 robustness and control rows (\texttt{analysis/results/clean\_reproduction.json}). The first attempt stopped at a 120\,s proof limit of the mixed-integer solver in one top-500 Condorcet stage (a timeout, not a mismatch and not proved infeasibility; no timeout is mapped to infinity); the same export was resumed with a 1200\,s limit, which changes no constraint or accepted status. The total of 14{,}758.4 s wall and 12{,}333.1 s CPU includes the failed stage and excludes idle and setup gaps; it is not an uninterrupted run. This pass uses the bundled pinned processed matrices; re-downloading and verifying the raw sources (\texttt{make data}) is a separate step that this recomputation did not repeat. The review and final additions were recomputed separately, each with zero numerical difference.
