\section{Related work}\label{sec:related}
\textbf{Regression adjustment for the global effect.}
\citet{chin2019regression} proposes the estimator studied here: fit the outcome on a unit's own treatment and functions of its neighbours' treatments, predict with every unit treated and with every unit control, and difference; he gives its properties under a linear model.
\citet{han2023model} add model-free graph and treatment covariates, sequential covariate selection and a block bootstrap, and \citet{eckles2017design} compare analyses that use neighbours' treatments, with cluster designs, in simulation.
\citet{sussman2017elements} develop estimation theory for network interference under a linear model, and \citet{basse2018limitations} study the limits of design-based inference under arbitrary interference.
\citet{hudgens2008toward} define direct and indirect effects under partial interference, \citet{savje2021average} show that standard estimators stay consistent for a marginalised effect under limited unknown interference, \citet{leung2022causal,li2022random} give asymptotics under approximate and random-graph interference, and \citet{ogburn2022causal,forastiere2021identification,basse2019randomization} treat observational networks and randomisation tests.
We do not re-derive the correctly specified theory. We ask what the error is made of when the fitted shape is wrong at the extrapolation point, and how it depends on the graph, the design and the mechanism.

\textbf{Exposure mappings and designs.}
\citet{aronow2017estimating} define Horvitz--Thompson and Hájek estimators over exposure conditions; they are unbiased when the exposure mapping is right and their variance grows as the probability of the condition shrinks.
\citet{ugander2013graph} randomise graph clusters so that full neighbourhood exposure becomes likely \citep[see also][]{karrer2021network,ugander2023randomized,leung2022rate}, \citet{cortez2022staggered} treat random subsets in stages and regress on the treated fraction, which places observations near the all-treated point but needs repeated measurements and no carry-over, \citet{optcov2023} optimise the covariance of assignments to reduce the variance of Horvitz--Thompson estimates, and \citet{yu2022estimating,shirani2024causal} estimate the total effect on an unknown network. We compare against them on the same draws, with graph convolutional networks \citep{kipf2017semi,defferrard2016convolutional} as the flexible regression.
