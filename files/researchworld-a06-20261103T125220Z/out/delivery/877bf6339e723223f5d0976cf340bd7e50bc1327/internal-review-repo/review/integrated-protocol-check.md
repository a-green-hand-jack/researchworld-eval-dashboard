## Statistical scope judgement

No substantive target or conditioning mismatch was found in the requested exposition.

1. The target is the chosen deterministic configuration's uniform-item accuracy over the 11,520 pinned questions outside the **entire** 512-question search reservoir. The 1,024-item disjoint audit estimates this target. Smaller conditional n/K slices keep that same complement. Neither the noisy audit mean nor the 12,032-item full-benchmark mean is substituted for the target.
2. The search count is hypergeometric over a uniform reservoir. Simultaneous tail inversion protects benchmark success totals for the fixed bank. Subtracting the observed search successes from the protected total endpoints and dividing by 11,520 transforms the event correctly, including after search-based winner selection. Coverage is unconditional over reservoir sampling, rather than conditional on each realized reservoir. Paper explicitly defines count endpoints; production inversion returns proportions and correctly multiplies them by 12,032 before this subtraction.
3. Conditional on the search reservoir and a winner frozen without audit outcomes, the fresh audit count is hypergeometric on the remaining population. Its exact interval has the stated conditional coverage. Paired wins/losses are marginally hypergeometric; the alpha/2 category union bound followed by opposite-endpoint subtraction is conservative without category independence. Additional nondefault-bank adjustment is correctly distinguished from a single prespecified selected contrast.
4. Simultaneous candidate audit intervals cover search-determined convex mixtures of remaining accuracies. The manuscript does not equate the finite Monte Carlo selection mixture with the exact infinite-repetition procedure expectation and does not claim that conditional MCSE removes audit uncertainty.
5. The separate iid paired-row calibration on all 1,536 cached rows has known empirical candidate means. Rerunning complete selection/reporting tests calibration against that empirical distribution, rather than establishing unknown-population or 11,520-item target coverage. The outer bootstrap is described as sensitivity, not an exact finite-design guarantee. Fixed-bank validity is not extended to feedback-generated candidates.

These are checks of the statements and implementation, not measurements of the main run or proof that its eventual exposure history follows the protocol. The generic iid audit argument in Appendix A remains separate from the explicitly finite design in Appendix F.
