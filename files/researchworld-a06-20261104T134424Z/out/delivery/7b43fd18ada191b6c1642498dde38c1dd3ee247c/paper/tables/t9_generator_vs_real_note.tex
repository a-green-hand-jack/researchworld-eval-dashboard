Gaussian-copula generator calibrated to the real matrix (accuracies as thresholds): one common latent correlation (``one-rho'', matched to the mean pairwise $\phi$) or one per pair (``pairwise copula'', tetrachoric correlations, 19 eigenvalues clipped). Gap rows: disjoint truth arm, mean $\pm$ s.e. (pp) of the generator (10 pools $\times$ 300 replications) with $z$ against the real replay in parentheses. Summary rows pool all $n$ (50, 100, 200, 400, 800) and both truth arms (disjoint and iid). The copula cannot reproduce the heterogeneity of the real pairwise dependence (near-duplicate and near-independent pairs) and, for the pairwise version, higher-order structure.
