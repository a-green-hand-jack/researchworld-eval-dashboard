Sensitivity of the width effect to the step budget, threshold and architecture. (a) Seeds 0–2 at widths 16 and 32 run with the 30,000-step ceiling (main) and with a 100,000-step ceiling (long; identical through step 30,000). Open triangles: not generalised at that ceiling (dotted lines). (b) Censoring-aware lognormal AFT slope of log t_gen on log2 width (negative: wider generalises sooner) over widths ≥ w_min, with 95% bootstrap CI over runs; right: censored/total runs. The old threshold-1.0 row is only a censored bound because those runs stop 500 steps after crossing 0.99; the exact row re-ran widths ≥ 64 to validation accuracy 1.0 (thr1). The lr·√(64/w) row (red, open marker) is the AFT slope over widths 16–256 with the learning rate scaled, seeds 0–2; 10 of 15 runs are censored, so its bootstrap CI collapses toward 0 and is not evidence of no effect; the row beneath is the fixed-lr slope on the same seeds and widths for reference.
