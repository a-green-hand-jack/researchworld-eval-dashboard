## Judgement

No substantive target, conditioning or two-design union mismatch was found in the changed manuscript.

The current primary target is accuracy outside the entire **retained 256-item search reservoir**, with denominator 11,776. Smaller n/K slices retain that complement. The 512 audit items estimate it with sampling uncertainty. The empirical calibration instead uses exactly 768 retained rows and four candidates; the 3,072 analyzed responses remain distinct from all 6,144 generated responses. Omitted candidate outputs are excluded from primary selection/calibration and retained in actual costs. Those changes are represented faithfully rather than silently carrying over the historical 11,520/1,536 Tier B targets.

Under the fixed C design, retained search and retained audit occupy disjoint fixed positions in the original uniform permutation. Given only the retained search identities, all assignments to other positions remain exchangeable. Every retained audit set of size 512 from the 11,776-item complement has the same number of completions into omitted original search/audit positions. Thus it is uniform after marginalizing those omitted identities. Conditioning on the old full search reservoir, or conditioning on the observed runtime trigger, would not justify this same argument; the text explicitly avoids both.

For each fixed design d, the search-derived simultaneous interval protects benchmark totals at candidate error alpha_d/K and transforms them by subtracting observed retained search successes and dividing by 12,032-s_d. Audit and selected paired-contrast intervals have their fixed-design conditional-on-search guarantees at error alpha_d; bank/mixture variants allocate additional candidate multiplicity within alpha_d. The primary selector is defined using that design's retained search labels and tie randomness, rather than using omitted labels or audit results. These statements give the required unconditional fixed-design failure probabilities before conditioning on tier choice.

Let F_B and F_C be the failure events of one specified endpoint family under the two fixed designs, on their common original-partition probability space. Each has probability at most .025. For **any** rule D choosing B or C, including a rule dependent on runtime and correctness-related variables, F_D is contained in F_B union F_C. Therefore its probability is at most .05. Independence between designs or between runtime and correctness is unnecessary. Different design-specific remaining-item targets do not break this argument: each F_d is defined using its own matching target and interval. It does not require generating the unchosen design's missing responses merely to calculate its counterfactual coverage event.

This is an unconditional guarantee over the original partition and any tier-choice randomness. It does not guarantee coverage or unbiasedness conditional on the realized trigger. The alpha allocation is separate for selected audit/gap, paired contrast, and simultaneous bank/mixture families, rather than a joint guarantee over all scientific endpoints. It does not extend to choosing an unprotected endpoint after inspecting results or to an additional unlisted design. These limits are stated in the manuscript. The original nominal 95% iid cache protocols and approximate bootstrap sensitivity remain distinct experiments.
