# ICLR 2027 version-review criteria

Adopted in response to internal-review-repo #3. Source: venue-repo `04606b8c82d89590a157d3625000ce6abe065cdb`, including `venue-requirements.md`, `INTEGRATION.md`, official template, captured official guidelines/AI policy and source manifest. The Research objective and frozen deliverable.md remain governing; venue compliance alone is not scientific approval.

At every proposed release/final head, check:

- Complete PDF and LaTeX/build inputs, mandatory scientific sections, reproducibility entry point and all required artifacts. The nonempty-path declaration is a presence test, not a validity test.
- Unchanged official ICLR 2027 style/BST, anonymous initial-submission format with `iclrfinalcopy` disabled, correct dimensions, legible final-size figures, figure captions below and table titles above. Verify actual rendered pages and style hashes; inspect every final figure again after resizing/integration.
- At most nine pages of main text, including abstract, figures and tables; do not misclassify essential results as statements or references. References excluded, appendices follow bibliography and are excluded. The explicit initial-submission rule and official template both say nine; the later conflicting FAQ's ten-page wording does not increase this study's limit.
- A truthful AI-use statement after main text and before references, at most one page, excluded from main count. Disclose AI roles in design, synthetic data/methods/proofs, implementation, analysis/interpretation, text, figures and literature work as applicable. Do not invent human authorship, manual checking or two-author verification. Distinguish studied LLM from research agents.
- Ethics and reproducibility statements are recommended by the venue. The objective independently requires detailed reproduction documentation. No separately attached checklist is established by the consulted ICLR2027 guidelines/policy/template; this conclusion excludes uninspected live submission forms and must change if a genuine additional official requirement is supplied.
- No actual conference submission, acceptance or completed submission-form declarations are implied.

Verification at adoption: independently checked all eleven captured-source/archive/extracted-file hashes and lengths in the manifest. Every one of the seven extracted official files matches its archive member byte for byte. Read the relevant captured author-guide rules and official sample AI-use section directly, not just venue's summary. Plot-label sizes of 8+ pt are recommendations, not a numerical official threshold; readability is judged on the actual delivered rendering. The official style has a 5.5-inch text width, so old seven-inch figure inspections do not certify a reduced version.

This record adopts concrete future checks. No integrated manuscript compliance or version approval is asserted.
