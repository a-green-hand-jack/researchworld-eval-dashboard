# Caption drafts (definitions only; the claims in the text are paper-repo's and must be checked against `results/`)

Common: "95% CI" = percentile bootstrap, B=200 (experiment-repo), resampling unit = target models for `extrap` (item-seed replicates averaged) and repetitions (independent model split + item seed) for `random`/`family`/`*_s*`. "Benchmark-level" CIs (cross-benchmark summaries) = 2000 resamples of the 13 benchmarks (HELM benchmarks share models, so these CIs are optimistic for them). `*` = development benchmark (predictor of MASS-Ens chosen after a pilot on its target errors). Budgets n = items evaluated on the target model. RMSE/MAE in accuracy units (0-1) unless stated; per-benchmark tables are in accuracy points (x100).

- `budget_relrmse.pdf` -- RMSE of each method divided by RMSE of the random-subset mean at the same n, per benchmark; line = geometric mean over 13 benchmarks, band = benchmark-level 95% CI; 20 repetitions (`random`: 20% random target models; `extrap`: strongest 20% as targets). `Best baseline by source CV` = per benchmark the baseline with the lowest RMSE in cross-validation on source models only (`best_baseline.csv`). `Sources only` (no target items) is clipped where it exceeds the axis range (large n).
- `budget_grid_{random,extrap}.pdf` -- per-benchmark RMSE vs n (log-log) with model-level (extrap) or repetition-level (random) 95% bands.
- `paired_rmse_{mass_ens,mass}.pdf` -- pre-declared headline (PLAN §4): per benchmark, RMSE(method)/RMSE(reference) at n=50 (filled) and n=100 (open) with the paired 95% CI (delta and CI from `paired.csv`, divided by the reference's point RMSE); bottom rows: geometric mean over all 13 benchmarks (`aggregate.csv`) and over the 9 non-development benchmarks (same definition, recomputed in `fig_paired.py`).
- `ess_gain.pdf` -- (RMSE_random / RMSE_method)^2 at the same n; thin = benchmarks, thick = median over benchmarks.
- `extrap_scatter.pdf` -- protocol `extrap`, n=50, one point per held-out target model, prediction averaged over 20 item-selection seeds, so the points show the systematic part of the error (bias), not the error of a single run; `bias` is the mean of (averaged prediction - truth) over the plotted targets, `RMSE (one run)` is the per-draw RMSE from `cells.csv` (as in the tables, includes item-selection variance; fractions, tables use points); identity line in black. `Sources only` is a constant and is omitted.
- `coverage.pdf` -- share of target predictions whose true score lies within +-1.96 reported SE of MASS-Ens (`coverage.csv`).
- `sensitivity.pdf`, `tables/robustness_n100.tex`, `tables/ablate_*_n100.tex` -- sensitivity/ablation runs use 10 repetitions and a subset of methods; RMSE relative to the random mean in the same protocol at n=100, geometric mean over the benchmarks run (all 13; `family` only the 7 Open LLM Leaderboard benchmarks).
- `tables/headline_<proto>_n<n>.tex` -- bold = not distinguishable from the best row of the column (paired benchmark-level bootstrap of the difference of log RMSE ratios / of Kendall tau_b has a 95% CI containing 0) -- NOT a significance claim against the others; `wins` = benchmarks with RMSE below the random mean (no multiplicity correction). `Sources only` uses no target items (constant prediction; rank metrics undefined, shown as --).
- `tables/perbench_*` -- bold = best point estimate in the row; dagger = paired 95% CI of (method - MASS-Ens) excludes 0 (`paired.csv`).
- `tables/data_audit.tex` -- from `data_audit.csv`; accuracy columns are x100 across models of the deduplicated matrix.
