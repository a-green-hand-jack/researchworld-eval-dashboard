# Data provenance and permissions

- OLLB contents: https://huggingface.co/datasets/open-llm-leaderboard/contents
  commit `9c09a7cae43334062a82cb164f2ef255013dafa2`; parquet SHA256
  `f13a7061e63133bc8745d89f8c7a56148584940ede22d3b27e21cad1e078dca5`.
- HELM: https://crfm.stanford.edu/helm/mmlu/latest/ ; scores from
  https://storage.googleapis.com/crfm-helm-public/mmlu/benchmark_output/releases/v1.13.0/
  (release summary dated2025-01-10). Individual source hashes in
  `data/provenance/helm_v1.13.0_sha256.json`; generated manifest hash in
  `data/processed/manifest.json`. All57 subject EM tables, not the aggregate
  MMLU score, form the matrix. Macro averaging is the primary study rule.
- One OLLB results JSON, solely for approximate item-count noise scales:
  commit `aa81ecc38fdc5708254b833923368970efdf5ef5`, path and hash recorded in
  `src/ollb_items.py` and `data/processed/ollb_noise.json`.

The downloads were made in the original run on2026-11-04 and retained through
the2026-11-08 checkpoint continuation. Anonymous public scores are used without
training or account access. Source score datasets do not declare a separate data
licence in the supplied material. We claim no licence over those scores; the
model-specific Hub License column and HELM code's Apache-2.0 licence do not
establish a score-data licence. Raw source files are not vendored. Derived score
matrices and checksums are supplied for research verification with attribution;
users reusing them should check the sources' current terms.

Deduplication keeps the highest reported Average among repeated fullnames,
which selects a variant rather than estimating a model's average performance.
All eligible processed matrices are finite and complete. No missing scores are
imputed; a HELM model would be excluded if any subject were missing (none are).
The OLLB source includes merged and fine-tuned models and selected submissions,
so independence and representativeness of model rows are not assumed.

No literature search was used to generate the analysis. The prohibited prior
study described in the objective was not sought or consulted by this position.

The released HELM `groups/mmlu.json` all-subject EM column was checked against
our 57-subject macro mean for every complete model using
`python3 src/audit_helm_aggregate.py RAW_HELM_DIR`. The audit and source checksum
are in `results/helm_aggregate_audit.json`; maximum discrepancy is below 1e-9
score points. We do not infer the interactive website's current sort state.

Duplicate selection is a convention, not a coordinatewise conservative rule.
`results/duplicate_selection_audit.json`, regenerated by
`python3 src/audit_duplicate_selection.py RAW_PARQUET`, verifies the pinned
parquet and compares highest- versus lowest-Average variants of every repeated
fullname. There are79 repeated names (79 discarded rows); for74 names the
highest-Average variant has at least one lower task score. Thus selecting it
cannot be claimed to only increase rivals' task scores or optimal investment
costs under all rules. Both targets and rivals change in this sensitivity;
the measured processing panels, rather than a dominance argument, support the
reported robustness. `make data` regenerates this audit.
