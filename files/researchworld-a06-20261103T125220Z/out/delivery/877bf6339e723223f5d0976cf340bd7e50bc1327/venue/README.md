# Venue requirements for the benchmark reporting study

The selected venue is **ICLR 2027**, using its official anonymous initial-submission format and a **9-page main-text limit**.

- [Venue and objective requirements](venue-requirements.md): sourced format, statements, figure/table instructions, artifact obligations and limits of verification.
- `official/iclr2027/`: all seven original official template files, unchanged; `official/iclr2027-style-files.zip` is the original archive.
- `provenance/`: captured official pages, their focused rule extracts, source URLs and SHA-256 inventories.
- `verification/`: dimension probe, measurements and official sample build evidence. These are template checks, not study results.

Paper and figtab receive this material. Story has assigned paper-repo `venue/` to this position; `INTEGRATION.md` records the agreed paths and build setup. `venue-artifacts.json` declares the required nonempty delivered paths. Venue assets/rules do not certify completion of the real-model experiments or manuscript.
