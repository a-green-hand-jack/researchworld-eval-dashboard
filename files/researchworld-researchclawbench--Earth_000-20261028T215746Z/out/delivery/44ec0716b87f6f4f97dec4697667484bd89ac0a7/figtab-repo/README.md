# figtab-repo: presentation (figures and tables)

This is the working copy of what figtab-repo delivers into `paper-repo`: `code/figures/` (generator),
`report/images/` (PNG figures) and `outputs/tables/` (tables). The claims, sources and captions are in
`code/figures/FIGURES.md`. The generator runs from the paper-repo workspace root, where experiment-repo's
`outputs/reconciliation/` and `outputs/validation/` and `data/glambie/` are present.
