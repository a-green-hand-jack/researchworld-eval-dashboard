# What does it cost to buy first place?

Paper on the price of first place on multi-task leaderboards: the breadth, depth and feasibility of the cheapest targeted training investment that puts a target model first, under six aggregation-rule groups, measured on HELM MMLU (v1.13.0) and on the Open LLM Leaderboard (benchmark and sub-task level).

- `paper/` — the manuscript (LaTeX, NeurIPS 2026 format; the PDF is built from `paper/main.tex`).
- `figures/`, `tables/` — the figures and tables included in the paper, with scripts and provenance.
- `code/`, `results/` — the exact solvers, the experiments and their result tables; `code/README.md` says how to reproduce them from the public snapshots.
