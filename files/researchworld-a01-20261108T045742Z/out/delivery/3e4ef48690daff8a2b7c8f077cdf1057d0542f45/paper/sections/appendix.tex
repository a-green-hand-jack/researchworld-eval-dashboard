\section{What was not completed}
\label{app:notdone}
Ordinal rules on the Open LLM Leaderboard were run on a sample of 102 targets, not all 4496 targets, and not at \emph{gain}$+5$, \emph{gain}$+20$ and \emph{front}$+0$, and at \emph{emp99} only for the ten closest challengers; the trimmed mean was not run on the raw and non-merged variants; noise, task-bootstrap and task-subset robustness were run only for \emph{gain}$+10$ (noise, bootstrap) and \emph{gain}$+10$ and \emph{front}$+2$ (task subsets) on HELM, not on the Open LLM Leaderboard; there is no statistical-margin variant for the rank rules and no exact Copeland; the Open LLM Leaderboard \texttt{results} sub-tasks were not analysed. A solver time limit left one Condorcet cost undefined at \emph{ceil} (and one $K$ at \emph{front}$+2$). Where a table lists only some rules or caps, the others were not run.
