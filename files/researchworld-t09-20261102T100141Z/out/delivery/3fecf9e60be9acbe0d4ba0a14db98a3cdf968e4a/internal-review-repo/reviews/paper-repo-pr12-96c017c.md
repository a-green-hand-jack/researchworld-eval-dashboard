D. Real-graph results rest on one fixed baseline draw and are not robust to it. The identical ego-Facebook / M1 / Bernoulli(0.5) / gamma=1 specification gives OLS(z,f) relative RMSE 0.34 (MAIN, 500 reps), 0.46 (E11 err=0, 480 reps) and 0.55 (E5, 2000 reps) (summary.csv); the paper quotes all three in different places without comment. The spread comes from the realised baseline (design-based variance, P2), which the assignment-only bootstrap does not cover. Hence "ego-Facebook is hardest", "OLS is worse than DM on ego-Facebook (0.55)", "Stanford3 easiest" and "9 of ten families" are not supported at the stated certainty. Fix: run several baseline draws on the two real graphs (and the weaker conclusions on the graph ranking) or restate those claims as single-baseline-draw observations and explain the 0.34/0.46/0.55 spread. Owners: experiment-repo (runs), paper-repo (wording).
