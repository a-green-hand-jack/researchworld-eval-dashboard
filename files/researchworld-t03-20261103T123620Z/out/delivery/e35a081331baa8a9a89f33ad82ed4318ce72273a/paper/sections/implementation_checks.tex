\section{Implementation checks and an unsuccessful control}
\label{app:implementation-checks}

These are completed development checks, separate from the main held-out reliability panel. Their raw records and executable checks are delivered with the analysis. They support the choice and numerical implementation of the reference; they do not establish main-panel adequacy, reference completeness or achieved runtime.

\paragraph{Common-game numerical check.}
The original prediction adapter could change low-order probability digits with batch shape when its inputs remained float32. The corrected adapter casts prediction inputs and probability accumulation to float64 for both approximations and direct enumeration. In \texttt{results/common\_game\_validation.json}, exhaustive Kernel SHAP and direct enumeration are compared on two pilot inputs for each of three model types on ACSIncome and Diabetes. All twelve comparisons pass the declared $10^{-12}$ maximum-coordinate-error check; the largest observed discrepancy is $4.44\times10^{-16}$ probability units. The generating script is \texttt{analysis/scripts/validate\_common\_game.py}. This checks two computational routes for the selected pilot games, alongside the analytic estimator tests; the completed main reference inventory separately passes its saved-vector audit (Section~\ref{sec:results}).

\paragraph{Exploratory TreeExplainer control.}
An attempted additional random-forest check used TreeExplainer with interventional probability output against archived direct-enumeration pilot references. All five checked inputs failed the declared $10^{-6}$ coordinate-agreement criterion. The observed maximum-coordinate discrepancies range from $1.06\times10^{-4}$ to $3.73\times10^{-4}$ probability units, and the TreeExplainer allocations also have nonzero efficiency residuals. Full vectors, baselines, residuals and failure flags are retained in \texttt{results/tree\_control\_validation.json}; \texttt{analysis/scripts/validate\_tree\_control.py} produces the diagnostic record. The initial strict check stopped on failure; the diagnostic version retains the unsuccessful comparisons. This control was not adopted as a reference. Its numerical or version compatibility was not resolved, so these observations support neither an independent validation by TreeExplainer nor a general claim about Tree SHAP accuracy. Direct enumeration remains the reference route for every main game.
