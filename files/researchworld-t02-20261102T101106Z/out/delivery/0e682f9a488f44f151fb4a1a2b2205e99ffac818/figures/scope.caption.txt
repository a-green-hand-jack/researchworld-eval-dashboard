Black: Kaplan–Meier median with 95% bootstrap CI; dashed grey: main-sweep medians for reference. (c) Learning rate scaled as lr·sqrt(64/width) (blue) against the fixed-lr main-sweep runs of the same seeds (grey); y-axis is t_gen, not delay, because most scaled-lr runs do not generalise. Points: seeds 0–4 at widths 32 and 128, seeds 0–2 at widths 16, 64, 256. Lines: Kaplan–Meier median of t_gen over seeds 0–2 in both arms with 95% bootstrap CI (sens_lrscale_grid.csv); an arrow marks a median that is undefined (at least half the seeds censored at the ceiling).
