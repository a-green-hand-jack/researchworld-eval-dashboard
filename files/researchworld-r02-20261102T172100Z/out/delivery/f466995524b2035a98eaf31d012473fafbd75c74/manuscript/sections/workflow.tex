\section{Research workflow and reproducibility scope}
\label{app:workflow}
The research workflow uses language-model agents for literature assessment, study planning, implementation, analysis, presentation and manuscript integration, with separate review functions. This is substantive assistance to the research process, beyond formatting alone. The numerical predictor and data generator are the explicitly specified regression and synthetic sampling procedures; they do not call a language model or a paid prediction API. Agent-generated code and reasoning require the same executable checks, evidence provenance and independent review as other research work. No agent identity is presented as a human author.

The supplied task restricts access to a withheld prior study and its derivatives. Background reading is deliberately bounded to allowed starter sources and directly unrelated official venue instructions, with no attempt to identify the withheld work. The reading provenance and consulted versions accompany the LaTeX source. Original sources that could not be obtained are not cited merely because they appear in the supplied list. This restriction limits an exhaustive novelty assessment and is not evidence that no other related work exists.
