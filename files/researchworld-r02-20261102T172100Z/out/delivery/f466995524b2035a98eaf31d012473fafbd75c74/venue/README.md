# Venue requirements and official template

This position supplies the verified **NeurIPS 2026 main-track submission** format for the compositional-output conformal reliability study. The complete paper has a 9-content-page limit, with references, optional appendix and the completed official 16-item checklist outside that count.

- `requirements.md`: objective and conference requirements, provenance, checks and unresolved evidence.
- `checklist-evidence-map.md`: factual evidence needed for each final checklist answer.
- `official/neurips2026/`: three unmodified official files, including the blank upstream checklist, not completed research material.
- `source-manifest.json`: URLs, acquisition outcomes, SHA-256 and frozen-input provenance.
- `sources/`: archived official instructions and retrieved formatting bundles.

Scientific claims and final checklist answers are delivered by their owners. This package does not claim acceptance, external submission, complete experiment execution, or licensing authority.
