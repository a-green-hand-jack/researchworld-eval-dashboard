\textbf{Joint response coverage under iid sampling.} Dots are means across 100 independent train/calibration/test replications; bars are pointwise 95\% $t$ intervals for these means, not simultaneous bands or intervals for a single calibrated run. Each replication uses 1000 training and 499 calibration observations and 5000 test observations per environment. The dashed line marks nominal $1-\alpha=0.90$. The target is the entire observed composition, not a class label. ``Coord. naive'' calibrates each coordinate at $\alpha$ and is a negative control; ``Coord. Bonferroni'' uses $\alpha/K$. Raw Euclidean and maximum regions are intersected with the simplex; Aitchison denotes the Euclidean norm of clr differences. The oracle weighted method uses unit weights under iid sampling. TV is exactly raw maximum at $K=3$ and is distinct at $K=10$; KL is forward $D_{\rm KL}(y\Vert\hat\mu)$.
