Curves describe observed finite-run fractions, not the distribution of known true per-input success probabilities. There are no confidence bands or individual-input certifications: even no observed failures in these runs does not establish a 0.95 success probability. Budget labels are requested work, with actual-work/time comparisons supplied separately.
