## 3. Risks and what to bound
1. **Volume in larger K (statistical risk).** Uniform-MC hit fractions fall as ~exp(-cK); for K>=10 and Aitchison-type balls the hit fraction is ~0 with M=5000 -> reported "volume" is 0 with huge relative error. Bound: report volume only for K<=5 by MC (K=3 also by the grid used in the prior work for a sanity check), and for larger K report a measure that does not need hit counts (threshold q in a score with a stated closed-form volume, e.g. Aitchison/ILR ball volume = Euclidean ball in R^{K-1}, or log-volume ratios). State the measure; do not rank methods on a measure that is below MC resolution. Never compare volumes across different reference measures.
2. **Zeros / boundary.** Log-ratio scores (CLR/ILR/ALR) are undefined at zeros; the answer depends on the replacement constant. Bound: either generate strictly positive synthetic y (Dirichlet-type) for the log-ratio comparisons, or make the replacement a pre-specified factor with a sweep eps in {1e-6,1e-4,1e-2}; for CIFAR (response = one-hot, y has zeros by construction) the composition-as-response reading collapses to a label problem. So: CIFAR belongs only to the label-set bridge (softmax = predictor output), with true label as the response, not to the simplex-valued-response study. Do not claim the simplex-region study on CIFAR.
3. **Conditional coverage cannot be estimated precisely.** With 1e3-1e4 test points and B=10 bins the binomial s.e. of a bin's coverage is ~sqrt(.09/ m_bin) = 0.03 at m_bin=100: worst-bin deviation is positively biased (max of noisy bins). Bound: use the synthetic oracle (known law -> Monte-Carlo conditional coverage given x) for the main conditional-coverage claim; on real data use a pre-specified bin count and compare to a null (shuffled-bin / exact-coverage simulation) so the bias is subtracted; do not report differences below the null spread.
4. **Full conformal / jackknife+ with refits.** Cost = refits per test point (full) or n refits (J+). [E] With a linear/softmax-link model (Dirichlet or multinomial-logit regression fitted by L-BFGS in <1 s) and n<=300 it is feasible (e.g. n=200 x 50 splits x 1 s = 3 h CPU of job time). With a neural net it is not. Bound: restrict full/J+/CV+ to the cheap predictor and small n, or drop full conformal and keep split vs CV+/J+ (K folds = 5-10 refits). Say it as a scope limit in the paper.
5. **Number of axes explodes.** Scores (~8) x calibration schemes (~4) x K (~3) x n (~4) x alpha (~3) x shift/predictor-miscalibration (~3) x 200 splits = >10^6 runs. Each is ms-level so the *grid* is feasible, but the *reporting* is not (figures/tables, text space of ~9 pages). Bound: choose 2-3 headline questions up front; everything else goes in the appendix as one table; fix alpha=0.1 for the headline, sweep alpha once.
6. **Theory.** The Beta law and the projection/Pythagoras argument (hierarchical-CP analogue) are cheap to state and check; a claim of a new theorem (e.g. exact coverage under the Aitchison score for a Dirichlet law, or inflation under label noise) is [E] a multi-turn risk. Bound: theory = (a) Beta(n+1-l,l) coverage law and its check; (b) at most one simple, fully proved proposition (e.g. a projection onto the affine hull cannot increase an L2/Aitchison-type score, hence regions don't grow, coverage kept) with a numerical check; flagged as a consequence of known results unless a genuine new step is proved. No "novel theorem" promise.
7. **Novelty claims.** The forbidden paper is not to be looked for; idea-repo reads related work for positioning. The paper must claim novelty only against read work; the survey section (objective step 1) is [E] ~1 turn of reading plus a table. Bound the survey to the ~12-15 sources idea-repo has actually read.
8. **Venue deliverables.** Latest ICML/ICLR/NeurIPS template and checklist must be fetched over the network and compiled (TeX Live is available [S]). [E] 1 turn for the venue position, then the paper position fills text; a template with unreadable-in-sandbox style files is a real-blocker risk: venue-repo should download and compile it in its first turn. Page limit ~9 pages main text; the number of figures must fit (see 5).
9. **Reproduction notes.** Every table/figure -> script map is required [S]; cost is small if the experiment scripts write one results file each and figtab reads only those. Bound: one `results/*.parquet|csv` per experiment family, one `make`/shell entry.

## 4. Recommended minimum viable study (what I judge can meet the objective)
1. Synthetic simplex-response study (K in {3,5,10}, Dirichlet-regression ground truth, known conditional law) with split CP; scores {L2, TV/Hellinger, Aitchison, Dirichlet-likelihood, projection variant}; metrics: marginal coverage vs Beta law (R=200 splits), oracle conditional coverage, volume (K<=5 by MC) or the measure of risk 1; calibration size sweep.
2. One calibration-scheme comparison (split vs CV+/jackknife+ vs Mondrian by argmax-component/group) on the same data, cheap predictor.
3. One real branch: CIFAR-10 softmax -> label-set methods (LAC/APS/RAPS) with the same metrics (SSCV / CovGap) and an entropy-stratified conditional coverage; plus one covariate-shift/corruption check only if budget remains.
4. Theory: Beta law + one proved proposition, each checked by simulation.
Everything else (full conformal with NN, many datasets, K>10 volumes, batch multivalid) is **out of scope** and must be listed as a limitation, not silently dropped.
