# Review 2 of the Brief (rev 2, wiki commit 2dd3a0e)

Verdict: all ten points of review 1 are addressed in the pushed text; the Brief is now a sound plan and I have no blocking objection. Four residual points, all about making the headline experiment and the pre-registered tests well defined. Checked on the frozen snapshot: OLLB `contents` has 550 models whose `Base Model` is on the board (509 fine-tuned/chat, 508 non-merged) as the Brief says.

1. **E2 needs a depth and B that are comparable across m.** Going from m=6 (benchmark level) to m~45 (sub-tasks) on the same models, ||delta||_1 and "B per task" change meaning: lifting a benchmark by B points is roughly lifting all its sub-tasks by B points (breadth and depth multiply by the number of sub-tasks), and a boost of B points on a 24-item sub-task is within its noise. Define the cross-granularity quantities before running E2: breadth as the fraction k/m, depth as the mean per-task boost ||delta||_1/m (or a boost budget in sigma units of that granularity), and say how a benchmark-level boost maps to its sub-tasks (all sub-tasks of the benchmark raised together, or sub-tasks free). R4's "breadth ratio rises with m" is only interpretable once this is fixed.
2. **Calibration is not representative of the targets.** Of the 550 resolvable pairs, 114 children have Qwen2.5-0.5B as base and the next bases are 3-14B models; the main targets are top-30 models, mostly 72B-78B. Report the gain distribution stratified by parent size and by base, with clustering by base model, and say that the B scale is transferred to large targets by assumption (or restrict the "in observed range" statement to the size bucket where pairs exist). Also note 571 rows have `Base Model` = "Removed".
3. **R3 and R1 need sharper definitions.** (a) The "aggregate gap" is in each rule's own units (and undefined as a scalar for plurality/Copeland); say whether R3 is tested within rule across targets, and use a rule-specific gap (e.g. aggregate difference to the incumbent, or rank gap for rank rules). As written, "gap explains the variance" does not by itself imply "the rule matters little". (b) R1 has no verdict for 60-80%; add the intermediate outcome ("partial support") so the pre-registration is complete. (c) Top-30 targets share incumbents and families, so they are not independent: use cluster bootstrap by incumbent/family for the paired CIs.
4. **Scope reminder, not a change.** The Brief has 5 propositions, E1-E7 and a calibration section; for 9 pages decide now which of E5, E7 and the spillover variant go to the appendix (suggestion: main text keeps E1-E4, E6 headline CIs and the budget curves).

Citation reminders unchanged: read Zhang & Hardt and Chen et al. fully before the related-work text is written; no forward-citation lookups.
