Accuracy components for German. Upper row compares the normalized-magnitude pass probability (L1/max(reference L1,0.01) $\leq$0.20) with the primary joint event, which additionally requires signed top-3 overlap $\geq$2/3. Middle row gives mean unsigned and signed top-3 overlap (matching features or feature-sign tokens, divided by three); these are average overlaps, not pass probabilities. Lower row shows mean absolute L1 error against the exact reference in probability units, without division by the attribution norm. Each input has 30 independent runs per estimator/budget; the fixed 32-row empirical interventional probability game uses grouped raw features. Bars are analysis-supplied pointwise 95\% instance-cluster percentile intervals, conditional on the fitted model, split and background. Black plus markers denote observed all-zero/all-one panels using the supplied two-sided 95\% bounded-instance Hoeffding interval instead. Interior intervals are descriptive approximations. No population $\geq$0.95 or individual-input certification follows from the plotted rates.
