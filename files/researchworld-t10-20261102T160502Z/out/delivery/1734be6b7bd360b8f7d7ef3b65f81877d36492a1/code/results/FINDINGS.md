# Findings from the final runs (R=2000, final seed space) — numbers from `results/summary_final_*.csv`

Reference M3 = `ppipp_pkg`. Ratio = paired geometric-mean width ratio vs M3 (<1 narrower). Coverage Wilson 95% CI half-width ≈ 0.01 at R=2000.

**C1 (calibration does not shorten CIs on real data).** M4 (`cal_iso`) is wider than M3 in 24 of 26 primary (dataset, n) cells; ratio 1.02–1.11 at n=50, 1.006–1.046 at n=200, 0.995–1.025 at n=400, 0.979–1.009 at n=1000. The only cells where it is narrower: gene_expression n=400 (0.995) and n=1000 (0.979), where Cov(Y,f)/Var(f)>1. The same cells are narrower for unclipped PPI++ (`ppipp_nc`: 0.979/0.974 at n=400/1000; census_income 0.986 for n≥200), i.e. the gain there is removal of the λ̂≤1 clip, not calibration. Floor from the population analysis (`theory_predicted_ratios.csv`, M4/M3 over all datasets, n, N): median 1.000, below 0.99 only for census_income (min 0.977) and gene_expression (min 0.904, mostly the clip); on the 45-cell synthetic grid the measured cal_iso/M3 ratio exceeds the floor-predicted ratio in every cell (mean log gap 0.07, range 0.01–0.27; log-correlation 0.88; `theory_check_synth_final.csv`) — the estimation cost of g.
**Dependence on N/n** (`summary_final_real_nsweep.csv`, cal_iso/M3): cost grows with N/n (n=100: galaxies 1.020 at N=n → 1.052 at 20n → 1.059 at 200n); gene_expression at n=400 falls to 0.970 at N=50n.
**C3 (small-n coverage).** M3 undercovers at n=50: alphafold 0.904, census_income 0.838, forest 0.923, galaxies 0.925; M5 `ppipp_jk` raises these to 0.954 / 0.868 / 0.942 / 0.949 for +10–15% width at n=50 (+2–4% at n=200). M5 on M4 (`cal_iso_t`) helps less (alphafold 0.880). census_income stays below nominal up to n=400 for all methods (skewed income labels is the suspected cause, untested; classical z also 0.874 at n=50) — a finite-sample/skewness problem not fixed by the jackknife-t.
**C4 (classical vs PPI).** M1/M3 width ratio 1.2–3.7 (ballots 3.6, gene_expression 2.85), PPI (λ=1) within ±2.5% of M3; ballots λ=1 is 2.5% wider at n=200.
**Shift (plankton, `summary_final_shift.csv`).** With 2013-labeled/2014-unlabeled, classical coverage → 0.00 at n=1000 (bias), M3/ppi/jk stay ≈0.95–0.96, but *every* λ̂-unclipped or calibrated variant collapses (n=400: cal_iso 0.49, cal_lin 0.50, ppipp_nc 0.49; no-shift control 0.93–0.94). Cause (checked directly, `src/shift_lambda_check.py` → `results/shift_lambda_check.csv`): under shift the pooled Var(f) shrinks (0.062 vs 0.114) so λ̂ ≈ 1.40 (>1 in 82–100% of draws; control 0.80), which puts negative weight on the labeled-minus-unlabeled prediction contrast that is biased by the shift; M3 survives only because the package clips λ̂ at 1 (E[Y−f] is stable across years: 0.003 vs 0.0045). Calibration itself is not the cause (cal_lin fails equally).
**Forest without replacement** (`summary_final_forest_wor.csv`, n=50/100/200): coverage M3 0.908/0.936/0.952 vs with-replacement 0.923/0.924/0.930 — no systematic difference within MC error; duplicates in the pool do not drive the forest results.
**Conformal baseline (C5).** Infinite interval for N=20n (see README); not simulated.
**Bootstrap ablation** `sim_boot.csv` (R=1000, n and N/n grid in file).
**Caveats.** Truth is the pool mean (finite population, draws with replacement); real-data cells are not independent across n (different seeds per cell, same pool). Multiplicity: headline claims are made on the 26 primary cells + 45 synthetic cells, with Wilson CIs per cell; no cell-level p-values are reported.
