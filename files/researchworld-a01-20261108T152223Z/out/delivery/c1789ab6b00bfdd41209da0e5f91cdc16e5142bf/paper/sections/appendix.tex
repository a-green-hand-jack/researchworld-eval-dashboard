\section{Solver bounds and validation}
\label{app:bounds}
\paragraph{Closed forms used.}
Let $\tau$ be the best rival's value under a score rule and $x$ the target's scores. \emph{Mean:} if the caps do not bind, the minimum $L_1$ is $n(\tau+\varepsilon)-\sum_t x_t$, whatever the allocation, and the minimum $K$ is the smallest number of tasks whose headrooms $c_t-x_t$ sum to at least this gap (a greedy cover by the largest headrooms). \emph{Median:} let $m$ be the best rival's median. The target's median exceeds $m$ as soon as $\lfloor n/2\rfloor+1$ of its scores exceed $m$ (for even $n$ both middle values are then above $m$), so, if the caps allow scores above $m$, $K\le\lfloor n/2\rfloor+1$; the bound is attained only if all but a minority of the target's scores are below $m$. \emph{Minimum:} the target's smallest score must exceed the best rival's smallest score, so every task below that value must be raised; $K\le n$. \emph{Power means ($p<1$):} at the optimum the lifted tasks share a common water level $y_t+s$ (up to the caps), computed by bisection. The validation in Table~\ref{tab:validation} checks these against independent references on real data.
