# Captions and what each item must show (for paper-repo)

**Evaluation (all items).** A new model's scores on benchmarks that were not run are predicted from the k that were. Error is z-RMSE: RMSE of (prediction - truth) divided by the training sd of each benchmark, pooled over target cells; 1.0 is the no-information per-benchmark training mean. The **target set is fixed across selectors and k** (MTEB: a random 25% of tasks per repeat; OLB and HELM Lite: leave-one-benchmark-out), so curves of different selectors and different k are comparable. **Full-information reference** (dash-dot line): all candidate benchmarks are given, same method; it is the floor attainable with these pools, not zero. Bands and brackets are 95% percentile cluster-bootstrap intervals (B=1000) over **test models** only (not training sets). Repeats of the random model split: MTEB 12 (36 test models each), OLB 5 (150), HELM Lite 20 (22). Pools: MTEB dense core 145 models x 254 tasks (about 190 candidates after the target split), OLB 4496 x 6, HELM Lite 91 x 10. Inputs and commit: `figures/inputs/PROVENANCE.md`; generator `figures/src/make_figs.py`.
