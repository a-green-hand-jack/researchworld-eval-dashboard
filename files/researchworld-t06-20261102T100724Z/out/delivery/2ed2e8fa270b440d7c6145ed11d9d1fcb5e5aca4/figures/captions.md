# Caption drafts (for paper/; descriptive, claims are the paper's to state)

**F1** Worst-case edit size to change a ranking claim, as a fraction of all comparisons ($k^*/N$, log scale), for the three perturbation families (P1 removals, P2 label flips, P3 added votes). Rows: C1 top-1 identity, C2 order of adjacent pairs (top 10), C4 a 95%-significant pairwise difference. Dot = median and bar = 2.5-97.5 percentile over replicate units of one dataset (Arena: 60 battle bootstraps; MT-Bench: 200 question-cluster bootstraps; NBA: 69 seasons) pooled over the claim instances; the black tick is the single full-data fit. $k^*$ is the smallest verified edit count found by greedy influence search with exact refits, hence an upper bound on the true minimum. Bars show variation across replicate units of each dataset, not a design-level confidence interval.

**F2** Random edits against the worst case. Curves: probability that the claim fitted on the full data no longer holds after $k$ uniformly random removals (solid) or label flips (dashed), with 95% Wilson bands (100-200 draws); vertical lines: worst-case $k^*$ for removals (solid) and flips (dashed); dotted horizontal: share of bootstrap resamples of all comparisons in which the claim differs from the full-data fit. Rows: C1, the adjacent pair at ranks 1/2 and the pair at ranks 2/3. A right-pointing marker at the end of a curve means no random edit changed the claim up to the largest size tried, so the ratio of that size to $k^*$ is a lower bound. NBA panel is season 2015 only.

**F3** The P1 result of F1 on four scales: absolute $k^*$, s1 $k^*/N$, s2 $k^*/N_{local}$ (comparisons involving the two participants of the claim), s3 the claim's gap in standard errors $z$ (dotted: 1.96). Same bars as F1; the order of designs can change between scales.

**F4** Size/sparsity control (s4). Arena subsampled to $N$ comparisons (line and band: median and 2.5-97.5 pct over 30 draws), Arena matched to MT-Bench (6 models, dense; 6 random models) and to an NBA season (30 models, sparse), against the real MT-Bench and NBA boards and the full Arena. Matched-subset bars are over draws; NBA dots are season medians. A real dataset lying on the matched-Arena marker at the same $N$ is not distinguished from Arena by size and sparsity alone.

**F5** Matched MT-Bench contrast: $\log(k^*_{GPT-4}/k^*_{human})$ over the same 200 question-cluster bootstrap replicates, for each claim instance (P1). Left: one random human judgment per key (equal $N$); right: all human judgments ($N$ about 1.85 times larger). Positive: the GPT-4 board needs more edits. Filled markers: 95% range excludes 0.

**F6** What predicts $k^*$. Left: $k^*$ against $z$ for C2 pair instances (800-point subsample per design, display only). Middle: log-log regression coefficients of $k^*$ on $z$ and $N_{local}$ (thin: bootstrap over replicate units, conditional on the datasets; wide pale: bootstrap over datasets as clusters, 12-72 clusters). Right: design shifts relative to crowd votes (units bootstrap only). Descriptive; not a causal model.

**F7** Validity of the search. Left: influence-predicted over exactly refitted change of the claim margin for edit budgets of 10%-100% of $k^*$ (P1). Middle: greedy against exhaustive $k^*$ on small instances (capped searches excluded); title gives the agreement count. Right: one-shot linear prediction against the verified $k^*$ on the full-data fits.

**F8** Crowd votes against three LLM judges on the same 49,938 single-turn Arena battles (only battles present in both datasets; all boards are refitted on them). Rows: C1 top-1 identity, C2 order of the pair at ranks 2/3 and 3/4, C4 significance of the 2/3 difference (ranks within each board's own ranking; the rank 1/2 pair is the C1 row). (a) Full-data worst-case removals $k^*$ (log scale), human (blue circle) and judge (orange triangle) joined by a line. (b) Paired log ratio $\log(k^*_{judge}/k^*_{human})$ over the same 40 battle-bootstrap resamples, median and 2.5-97.5 percentile; upper bar P1 removals, lower bar P2 flips; negative: the judge board needs fewer edits; filled marker: the range excludes 0. (c) Unperturbed $z$ = gap / s.e. of the same pair (dotted: 1.96). Tie rates differ between the human votes (31%) and the judges (Llama-3-70B 5%, Athene-70B 4%, Qwen2-72B 26%), so judge boards are not human boards with a different scorer; the 40 resamples support only the within-dataset range shown, not a design-level interval.

**T1** Boards: comparisons $N$, participants, comparisons per participant, tie rate, score s.d. (BT log scale). **T2** $k^*$ per dataset, claim and family: full-data fit (range over claim instances), and median with 2.5-97.5 percentile over replicate units. **T3** Ratio of $k^*$ under a modelling variant to the reference fit for the same claim instance (IQR over instances); pooled NBA windows are different boards and are reported as $k^*/N$.
