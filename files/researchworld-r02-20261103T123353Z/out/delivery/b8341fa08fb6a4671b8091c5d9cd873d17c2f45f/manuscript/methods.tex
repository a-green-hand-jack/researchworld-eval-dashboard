\section{Implemented comparisons and controlled mechanisms}
\label{sec:methods}
\paragraph{Score families and controls.}
All methods share a positive fitted center $m(x)$. With $B\in\R^{K\times(K-1)}$ satisfying $B^TB=I$ and $B^T\mathbf1=0$, define $z(y)=B^T\log y$ on the positive simplex \citep{egozcue2003ilr}. The implemented scalar scores are
\begin{align}
 s_{\rm TV}(x,y)&=\tfrac12\|y-m(x)\|_1,&
 s_{\rm L2}(x,y)&=\|y-m(x)\|_2,\\
 s_{\rm H}(x,y)&=\tfrac1{\sqrt2}\|\sqrt y-\sqrt{m(x)}\|_2,&
 s_{\rm ILR}(x,y)&=\|z(y)-z(m(x))\|_2.
 \label{eq:scores}
\end{align}
Each score uses Equation~\eqref{eq:rank}. Coordinate controls instead calibrate $|Y_j-m_j(X)|$ separately and intersect all resulting intervals with $\simplex$, either at error $\alpha$ (naive) or $\alpha/K$ (Bonferroni). Naive calibration controls individual coordinates, not the desired joint event. Heterogeneous cells additionally use $s_{\rm ILR}(x,y)/\widehat\sigma_{G(x)}$ with training-only scales, or separate ILR thresholds for each fixed group $G\in\{0,1\}$ (Mondrian). Thus comparisons isolate score geometry, scaling, and calibration granularity rather than different fitted centers.

For actual-zero responses, unsmoothed ILR is recorded as inapplicable to the entire mechanism. The operative ILR-smoothed score replaces both arguments by
\begin{equation}
 T_\epsilon(y)=\frac{y+\epsilon\mathbf1}{1+K\epsilon},\qquad \epsilon>0.
 \label{eq:smoothing}
\end{equation}
The region consists of original candidates $y$ whose transformed score is below threshold. It includes the original zero-valued responses in its coverage denominator. The primary $\epsilon=.001$ and sensitivities $10^{-6},.01$ define different scores; their location fit is held fixed. Finite-threshold full-domain status and infinite-threshold frequency are distinct diagnostics. Where full-domain status for smoothed ILR cannot be certified, it is recorded as unknown rather than inferred from an estimated area near one.

\paragraph{Predictor and data mechanisms.}
The covariates are $X=(u,v,G)$, with independent $u,v\sim\mathrm{Uniform}[-1,1]$ and $G\sim\mathrm{Bernoulli}(.1)$. A deterministic feature/coefficient rule generates a positive base center $\mu(X)$ (Appendix~\ref{app:generators}). Five mechanisms contrast: (i) isotropic logistic-normal noise of scale $.45$ in ILR coordinates; (ii) group-heterogeneous logistic-normal scales $.25$ and $1.25$; (iii) component-0 logit shifted by $3$ with logistic-normal scale $.8$; (iv) a Dirichlet distribution of total concentration $15$ around a floored version of the shifted center; and (v) logistic-normal scale $.8$, followed independently with probability $.25$ by setting one uniformly selected component exactly to zero and renormalizing. These are specified generative mechanisms, not estimated characteristics of a real dataset.

A trial draws 2,000 proper-training observations, 499 calibration observations and 2,000 tests. The first 1,500 training observations fit ridge regression of $z(Y)$ on $(1,u,v,\sin(\pi u),uv,G)$ with penalty $.001$ and an unpenalized intercept. In the zero mechanism only, the regression target uses $T_{.001}(Y)$. Inverse ILR gives the shared positive center $m(x)$. The remaining 500 training observations fit groupwise RMS ILR residual scales; an absent group uses overall RMS, with floor $10^{-8}$. No calibration or test response fits a location or scale. This center estimates a log-ratio location, generally different from the Euclidean conditional mean. Geometry conclusions are conditional on this shared predictor rather than per-score optimally chosen models.

\paragraph{Prespecified study matrix and contrasts.}
The pre-outcome frozen manifest specifies 19 cells and 100 independently regenerated/refitted trials per cell. Ten core cells cross five mechanisms with $K=3,10$. At $K=3$, interior and heterogeneous mechanisms additionally use calibration sizes $99$ and $1{,}999$ and nominal error $.05$. Two zero-mechanism cells vary $\epsilon$, and one heterogeneous stress cell changes test-group prevalence from $.1$ to $.4$. This stress preserves the complete within-group law: it is a specific covariate shift, not a change in $Y\mid X$. Fixed-group calibration retains its within-group and mixture guarantee for this change; unweighted global calibration has no such general protection (Appendix~\ref{app:rank}).

The declared contrasts compare TV/L2/Hellinger with ILR, naive coordinates with Bonferroni, and global ILR with scaled and Mondrian ILR in heterogeneous cells. Sensitivities compare with their matched base cell. All 19 cells and their prescribed numerical audits completed; pilots are excluded from confirmation. The scientific seeds are offset by $10{,}000{,}000$ from pilot seeds; all methods within a trial share partitions, and matched sensitivity arms share the declared seed. Exact cell seeds, score applicability, and execution provenance are machine-readable under \texttt{analysis/}.

\paragraph{Efficiency estimation and interpretation policy.}
Common area is measured only at $K=3$; $K=10$ supports coverage and applicability without a high-dimensional volume ranking. For each fitted trial, area is evaluated at the first 32 test locations in each observed group. Test order is independent of outcomes, so these form groupwise random subsets. Their means are weighted by actual test-group proportions. An absent test group contributes zero weight; a missing area in a positive-weight group remains undefined. Transformed-region integration uses $2^{14}$ scrambled Sobol points with the common-simplex Jacobian (Appendix~\ref{app:integration}). Trial/group/location keys separate integration randomness across trials and preserve numerical pairing across methods. Integration maps still differ across geometries.

Outer-trial summaries use means, trial SDs and two-sided Student-$t$ 95\% intervals, with 5,000-draw percentile trial-bootstrap sensitivity intervals (seed 90210). Paired contrasts use within-trial differences. These intervals include changing fits, calibration samples, tests and area-location subsets; they do not bound shared integration bias. A separate, prespecified audit recomputes matched locations at $2^{16}$ points with four scrambles. An empirical numerical allowance enlarges paired statistical intervals (Appendix~\ref{app:integration}); it is not a uniform numerical confidence bound. Missing audits or enlarged intervals crossing zero leave an area ranking unresolved. Pre-outcome practical margins are $.01$ in coverage and $.005$ in normalized area; smaller effects do not establish a useful ranking. Minority-coverage mean half-widths above $.02$ are flagged as imprecise. These are study interpretation choices, not field-wide standards or equivalence tests.
