Report the actual stopping rule and distinguish a fixed-design/marginal guarantee from any stronger conditional claim; assess whether a defensible independence argument or narrower claim is needed. This is a claim/validity issue for analysis. Proceed with cache checks and the full required analyses; this risk does not justify restoring expensive Tier B. Feasibility clearance does not certify an exact post-stopping guarantee.

For N=11,776 and audit512, the worst-case finite-population planning SE is 2.1612 percentage points; an approximate normal 95% half-width is **4.2360 points**. These are planning calculations, not observed exact error bars. Precision is materially weaker than Tier B. Pairwise improvement precision depends on discordance and can be worse; a small real gap or superiority comparison may remain inconclusive. That is an acceptable research finding with full evidence. Twenty thousand cache repetitions reduce conditional Monte Carlo noise, not fresh-question uncertainty. Keep empirical-cache known truth, synthetic known truth and genuine audit uncertainty distinct.

Retain v2's limits: one small quantized model, a strict direct-letter parser, a fixed outcome-independent bank, a finite pinned benchmark and limited source access.
