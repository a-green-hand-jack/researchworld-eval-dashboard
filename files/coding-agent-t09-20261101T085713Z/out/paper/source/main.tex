\section*{Impact Statement}
This paper studies statistical methodology for online experiments using synthetic outcomes and public, anonymised graphs; we see no specific negative societal consequences beyond those of A/B testing in general. Mis-estimating spillovers can lead to wrong product decisions, which is why we emphasise error bounds and failure conditions.
