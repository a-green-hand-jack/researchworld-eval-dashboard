# Results guide (analysis position)

All files are produced by scripts in `scripts/` from code in `src/cpc/`; every row is a measurement from a run in this repository.
Replicate-level CSVs are *long format*: one row per (configuration, method, `rep`). `*_summary.csv` are made by `scripts/aggregate.py` (mean over `rep` with 95% t-interval, columns `<m>`, `<m>_lo`, `<m>_hi`).
Re-run map and environment: see `REPRODUCE.md` (added at the end of the study).

## Common definitions
- Target miscoverage alpha = 0.1 is primary (`cov10`); `cov05`, `cov20` are alpha = 0.05 / 0.2. Thresholds: split-conformal, the ceil((n+1)(1-alpha))-th smallest calibration score (`inf` if rank > n).
- `rep`: independent replicate = fresh data draw, predictor fit, calibration split and test set (seed = `seed0 + rep`, `seed0 = 0`). Uncertainty is the spread over `rep`; replicates are independent, so a t-interval for the mean over `rep` is valid. Test-set noise in each `rep` is ~ sqrt(.09/2000) = 0.007 for `cov10` (E1).
- Method names: `l2, l1 (=TV), hell, kl (KL(y||yhat)), ait (Aitchison = L2 on CLR after additive zero replacement eps then closure), linf, nll (negative Dirichlet log-density, precision fitted by MLE on the sigma split)`; suffix `_n`: score divided by a locally estimated scale sigma(x) (random forest on log-scores, held-out sigma split; `nll` has none because it can be negative); `_m`: Mondrian split by argmax of the predicted composition; `cvp_l2`: CV+ (K=10) with the L2 score on the pooled fit+calibration data (linear predictor only; uses 1500 points for fit+calibration, others use 1000 fit + 500 calibration).
- Zero handling: `eps` (default 1e-3) enters only `ait`, `nll` (and `kl` for yhat); l2/l1/hell/linf are defined with exact zeros.

## E1 synthetic compositional regression (`results/e1/`)
Files: `main_<family>_D<D>_h<0|1>_<pred>.csv` (4 families x D in {3,5,10} x hetero in {0,1} x pred in {linear, rf}; 100 reps each, `rep` 0-99; extension files `main_*_s100.csv` with `rep` 100-199 are added if budget allows), `sens_eps<eps>_<family>.csv` (D=5, hetero=1, linear; 100 reps; eps in {1e-4,1e-2,1e-1}), `sens_ncal<n>_<family>.csv` (D=5, hetero=1, linear; 100 reps; n_cal in {30,100,250,2000}). Summaries: `results/e1_main_summary.csv`, `e1_sens_eps_summary.csv`, `e1_sens_ncal_summary.csv` (columns as below, with `_lo/_hi`).
Generator (`src/cpc/data.py`): x ~ N(0, I_4); mean mu(x) = softmax(Bx + b), fixed per configuration (seed 0); `dirichlet`: y ~ Dir(phi mu), phi=20/h(x); `lognormal`: y = softmax(log mu + sd h(x) eps), sd=0.5; `multinomial`: counts/n, n=30 (hetero: n = round(30/h) >= 5), exact zeros; `rounded`: lognormal with parts < 0.01 set to exactly 0 and renormalised. h(x)=exp(0.8 w.x) for hetero=1 (noisier where h large), else 1. Predictors: `linear` (multinomial-logit on soft labels = correct mean model for dirichlet/multinomial, misspecified for lognormal/rounded) and `rf` (RandomForest on y). n_fit=1000, n_sigma=500, n_cal=500, n_test=2000.
Columns:
- `cov05/cov10/cov20`: test-set marginal coverage. Validity claim: mean over rep within Beta band (see theory T1; expected mean in [1-alpha, 1-alpha+1/(n+1)] for continuous scores).
- `ymax_min/ymax_max`: min / max coverage over 5 equal-mass bins of the predicted largest component; `ent_min/ent_max`: same, bins of predicted entropy; `scale_min/scale_max` (hetero=1 only): 3 equal-mass bins of the *true* noise factor h(x). Gap = max - min (`ymax_gap`, `ent_gap` in the summary). Bin noise ~ sqrt(.09/400) = 0.015 per bin, so a gap below ~0.05 is not distinguishable from 0.
- `or_gap`, `or_rmse`, `or_q05`, `or_q95`: **oracle pointwise conditional coverage.** For 60 test points x_i, 400 fresh draws of y | x_i from the true law give c_i = P(y in C(x_i) | x_i, calibration set); `or_gap = mean_i |c_i - 0.9|`, `or_rmse` the root-mean-square deviation, `or_q05/q95` the 5%/95% quantiles of c_i. MC noise of one c_i is 0.015 (a positive bias of ~0.012 in `or_gap` even for a perfect method). Main conditional-validity measure on synthetic data. Not available for `cvp_l2`.
- `bnd_frac`, `bnd_cov`, `int_cov`: coverage conditional on the **outcome** (min component of y < 0.01 vs not). This is conditioning on y, not x, so no conformal method controls it; reported as a diagnostic of which part of the simplex a score under-covers (`bnd_frac` is the share of test points that are boundary-near).
- `wsc`: worst-slab coverage (delta=0.1 mass, 200 random directions in x-space; slab chosen on one half of the test set, evaluated on the other half; Romano et al. 2020 style). Single number per rep; use spread over rep.
- `vol_med`, `vol_mean`: Lebesgue volume of region ∩ simplex, as a **fraction of the simplex volume** (Monte Carlo, 4000 uniform Dirichlet(1) points, median / mean over 60 test points; MC noise <= 0.008). Only D in {3,5}; `NaN` for D=10 (fraction is ~0). Compare only at equal coverage (all methods are marginally 0.9). Reference measure = Lebesgue on the simplex; Aitchison measure would rank differently.
- `pool_frac`: **dimension-valid efficiency measure**: mass of the *marginal* law of y inside the region, i.e. fraction of a pool of 300 fresh draws of y (from the generator, x ~ N(0,I)) falling in C(x_i), averaged over the same 60 test points as `vol_*`. Defined for every D (incl. D=10, where `vol_*` is NaN) and identical in definition for all scores; lower = more informative at equal coverage. Reference measure = the marginal law of y (differs from `vol_*`, which uses Lebesgue). For D<=5 it ranks like `vol_*` except where the marginal mass concentrates near the boundary.
- `inf_frac`: share of test points with an infinite threshold (Mondrian groups with too few calibration points). `phi_hat`: fitted Dirichlet precision (used by `nll`).
Claims supported (pre-specified comparisons, paired by `rep`): (C1) all methods are marginally valid; (C2) plain scores differ strongly in conditional coverage (`or_gap`, `ymax_gap`, `wsc`) and efficiency (`vol_med`); (C3) locally-normalised and Mondrian variants reduce conditional gaps; (C4) the best score depends on the noise family (Aitchison for lognormal noise vs Hellinger/KL for count-type noise) and on exact zeros; (C5) Aitchison-type scores are sensitive to eps when y has zeros (`sens_eps*`); (C6) small n_cal widens the coverage distribution as the Beta law says (`sens_ncal*`).

## E2 classification with simplex scores on CIFAR-10 (`results/e23/e2_*.csv`, `*_ident.csv`)
Predictor: small CNN trained by `scripts/train_cnn.py` (12 epochs, test accuracy 0.8806; logits at epoch 2 (acc 0.7403) and 12 saved in `cnn_logits_ep*.npy`; labels in `cifar10_test_labels.npy`). Calibration/test = random split of the 10 000 CIFAR-10 test images, `n_cal` in {100,500,1000,5000}, 200 reps; `T` = softmax temperature applied to saved logits. Sets: {k: score(e_k, p) <= q}. Methods: `lac` (1-p_k), `aps` (randomised), `raps` (lambda=0.01, k_reg=2, randomised; not tuned), `l2` (Brier), `l1`, `hell`, `kl`, `ait3/ait1` (Aitchison with eps 1e-3 / 1e-1), `lac_cc` (class-conditional LAC).
Columns: `cov*`, `size*` (mean set size), `classcov_min/mean` (per-class coverage), `sscv_min` (min coverage over set-size strata {1},{2-3},{4+} with >=30 points; Angelopoulos et al. SSCV), `empty_frac`, `conf_min/conf_max` (5 bins of top-1 probability), `wsc` (slabs in log-prob space), `maxsize_frac_all`.
`*_ident.csv`: share of test points whose set equals LAC's, per method (claim T2: on vertex labels hell/kl/l1 sets are *identical* to LAC since the scores are monotone in p_y).

## E3 CIFAR-10H soft labels (`results/e23/e3_*.csv`)
Target = CIFAR-10H human label distribution (10 000 test images, 80.6% exact zeros), prediction = CNN softmax (T=1). Each rep: random split sigma (2000) / calibration (`n_cal`) / test (rest). Columns as E1 plus `ambig_min/ambig_max` (coverage in 5 bins of the entropy of the human distribution; an outcome-conditional diagnostic) and `pool_frac`: mean fraction of *other* images' human distributions falling inside the region (100 test points x 300 pool images): an informativeness measure valid for any dimension (lower = more informative at equal coverage).

## Theory checks (`results/theory/`)
- `t1_beta_law.csv`: for family x score x n in {19,50,100,500}: empirical mean/sd/5%/95% quantiles of the conditional coverage over 3000 fresh calibration sets (test pool of 2e6) vs the Beta(k, n+1-k), k = ceil((n+1)(1-alpha)) law, with KS statistic and p-value. Claim T1.
- `t4_cond_coverage.csv`, `t4_points_<score>_phi<phi>.csv`: delta-method (large-precision Gaussian) prediction of the pointwise conditional coverage `pred` vs simulated `emp` (oracle mean predictor, Dirichlet noise) for l2, ait (first-order), ait2 (exact first two moments of clr of Dirichlet), hell; 300 x's per phi. Claim T4: which score's conditional coverage is predicted to be homogeneous.

## E4 covariate shift (`results/e4/shift_<family>.csv`, `results/e4_summary.csv`)
D=5, hetero=1, linear predictor; calibration x ~ N(0,I), test x ~ N(delta w, I) with w the unit noise direction of the world (shifts toward noisier inputs), delta in {0,0.5,1,1.5}; 100 reps. `weighted=1`: weighted split conformal with the exact likelihood ratio (Tibshirani et al. 2019, per-test-point threshold); `weighted=0`: ordinary split conformal. `ess`: effective calibration sample size (sum w)^2/sum w^2. `cov10` is coverage on the shifted test set. Claim C7: unweighted scores lose coverage under shift, with the plain scores much worse than the locally normalised ones in Dirichlet/lognormal families; weighting restores ~0.9; in multinomial the normalised Aitchison score over-covers. Script: `scripts/run_shift.py` via `scripts/e4_shift.sh`.

## Null simulation of the binned metrics (`results/null/null_binned.csv`, script `scripts/run_null.py`)
Claim C5. 5 values of `n_cal` x 500 reps. Conditional coverage is exact by construction: per rep the coverage level p ~ Beta(k, n+1-k) (exact split-conformal law), indicators Bernoulli(p) on 2000 test points independent of features; the same `binned_gap`/`wsc` code as E1. Columns as E1 (`ymax_*`, `ent_*`, `scale_*`, `ymax_gap`, `wsc`, `or_gap`, `or_rmse`) plus `p_cond` (the true coverage level of that rep). Read: the E1 value of a metric is interpretable only if it lies outside the null spread (e.g. 95% null quantile of `ymax_gap` or 5% null quantile of `wsc` at the same n_cal; E1 uses n_cal=500). `or_gap` has an MC floor (0.016 at 400 draws x 60 points, n_cal=500).

## Notes
- For D=3 `l1` and `linf` are the same score up to a factor (the three differences sum to zero, so TV = L-infinity), so their rows coincide there; for D>=4 they differ.
- Scheme mapping for the n_cal sweep (C4): method `X` = split, `X_m` = Mondrian (argmax of prediction), `cvp_l2` = CV+; `X_n` = locally normalised split.
- E3 ties: CIFAR-10H vote proportions are discrete (80.6% exact zeros, 50-ish voters per image), so scores tie; with ties only the lower bound 1-alpha is guaranteed for the split threshold. Coverage there refers to the noisy vote proportions, not a latent label law.
- Licences: CIFAR-10 (Krizhevsky 2009, no licence stated, cite the technical report; copy used: Hugging Face `uoft-cs/cifar10`, revision 0b2714987fa478483af9968de7c934580d0bb9a2, card license `unknown`); CIFAR-10H (Peterson et al. 2019; github.com/jcpeterson/cifar-10h, file `data/cifar10h-probs.npy`, 800128 bytes; no licence file stated in the repo as retrieved).
- Superseded: the first 100-rep pilot grid (without `or_*`, `pool_frac`) and a stopped 200-rep attempt were deleted and regenerated with the final code; the 12-config theory run with the first `ait2` bug (Cholesky failure) was rerun.

## Final E1 grid: what it shows (100 reps per config, 48 configs; 95% t-intervals over `rep`)
- Marginal coverage at alpha=0.1 lies in [0.896, 0.910] for every config and method (C1): all methods are marginally valid.
- Conditional coverage is where methods differ. Mean oracle gap `or_gap` / worst-slab coverage `wsc` (mean over all configs): plain l2 0.090/0.73; locally normalised l2_n, hell_n, kl_n, linf_n 0.07-0.08/0.83-0.84; plain Aitchison 0.110/0.65 (worst). Under heteroscedastic noise plain l2 has `wsc` 0.60 and `or_gap` 0.118, normalised l2_n 0.82 and 0.083 (C3). Normalisation narrows but does not remove the gap, and it costs `pool_frac` (see E3).
- The null band (`results/null/null_binned.csv`, correct-conditional-coverage by construction) is `or_gap` 0.016 and `wsc` 0.90 at n_cal=500: every method's `or_gap` is far above it, so the gaps are not sampling noise. Homoscedastic worlds also show `or_gap` ~0.06 for l2 because the score's spread depends on the predicted mean (T4).
- `pool_frac` vs `vol_med` (D<=5, non-NLL methods): Spearman rank correlation across methods within a config averages 0.89 (D=3, min 0.69) and 0.96 (D=5, min 0.81), so `pool_frac` is a valid proxy where `vol_*` exists; at D=10 it is the only efficiency measure. At D=10, h=1, linear: l1/kl/hell/nll are the most informative (pool_frac 0.06-0.2), linf and plain Aitchison the least (0.15-0.34).
- Sensitivity (`e1_sens_*`): Aitchison marginal coverage is invariant to the zero-replacement eps but its `pool_frac` falls from ~0.5-0.6 (eps=1e-4) to ~0.14-0.3 (eps=0.1) in the three families; Mondrian-by-argmax over-covers at n_cal=30 (~0.96) and reaches 0.90 at n_cal=2000.
