\textbf{Subgroup coverage and controlled mixture shift.} Dots are means across 100 independent train/calibration/test replications; bars are pointwise 95\% $t$ intervals for these means, not simultaneous bands or intervals for a single calibrated run. Each replication uses 1000 training and 499 calibration observations and 5000 test observations per environment. Circles and squares are iid coverage in the prespecified lower-noise group 0 and higher-noise group 1. Triangles are marginal coverage after the test group-1 probability changes from $0.2$ to $0.8$, with within-group laws preserved. Colors and shapes distinguish estimands; shifted marginal coverage is not a third subgroup. ``Group Aitchison'' calibrates within observed groups; ``Oracle weighted'' uses the known density ratio, unavailable in general applications. The dashed line marks 0.90. TV exactly duplicates raw maximum at $K=3$; KL is forward $y\Vert\hat\mu$. This experiment does not test arbitrary shift.
