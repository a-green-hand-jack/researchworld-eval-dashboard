Pilot budget accounting, not main-study reliability evidence. Each line joins two measured calls for one of two pilot inputs per fitted model, with a fixed 32-row empirical background and grouped raw-feature players. The horizontal axis is the requested coalition-work setting; the vertical axis counts actual input rows evaluated by the prediction pipeline. Colors, markers and line styles identify the estimators. Hollow Kernel SHAP markers denote exhaustive evaluation over the effective varying players; complete permutation-cache coverage does not imply an exact path-sampling estimate. Overlapping calls may obscure one another. Traces for the three fitted models coincide in prediction-row count; the accompanying table shows their distinct CPU costs. These pilot calls have no population uncertainty interval. They motivate measuring actual work and using dimension-specific stochastic budgets in the subsequent study.
