\subsection*{Ethics statement}
All data are public scores; no model is trained and no human subjects are involved. The study could inform strategic gaming of leaderboards, but it is intended to help benchmark designers judge how aggregation choices shape incentives, and it makes no claim about any developer's practices.
\subsection*{Reproducibility statement}
Code, pinned processed tables with SHA-256 checksums, seeds, commands, measured run times (Appendix~\ref{app:repro}), a table/figure-to-script map and the status ledger are in the supplement (\texttt{analysis/}, \texttt{README.md}); data sources and licences are in Appendix~\ref{app:data}.
