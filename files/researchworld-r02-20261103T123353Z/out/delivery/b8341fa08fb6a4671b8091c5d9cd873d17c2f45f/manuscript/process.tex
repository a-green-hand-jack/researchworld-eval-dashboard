\paragraph{Impacts, ethics and assets.}
Separating marginal from group reliability may help detect false reassurance. An adverse use would be treating a nominal guarantee or favorable synthetic result as a deployment safety certificate, especially for poorly covered minorities or changed populations. We delimit target/population claims and distinguish coverage from fairness or decision benefit. These are anticipated implications, not measured downstream effects. There are no human participants, personal datasets or high-risk pretrained models. The NeurIPS Code of Ethics at \url{https://neurips.cc/public/EthicsGuidelines} was read on 2026-11-03; relevant principles concern representative evaluation, transparency, attribution and reproducibility. Official style assets remain unchanged and attributed in the venue record; no separate archive license grant was supplied, so none is invented. Dependency/font and generated-asset terms are recorded with their locally verified scope in the reproduction package.
