One-run adequacy: normalized L1 error <=0.20 and signed top-3 overlap >=2/3 against the exact finite-game reference. L1 normalization is max(reference L1,0.01 probability units). For each fixed fitted model, 60 held-out inputs and 30 independent runs per input, estimator and requested budget; background B=32. The target is positive-class probability for the fixed empirical interventional game over grouped raw features. Estimates average equally over inputs and runs. Bars are analysis-supplied pointwise 95% instance-cluster percentile intervals, conditional on the fitted model, split and background. Black plus markers identify observed all-zero/all-one panels whose displayed bars instead use the supplied two-sided 95% bounded-instance Hoeffding interval. These are observed rates and descriptive intervals, not population 95% adequacy certification. Horizontal positions are mean measured per-call process CPU time, excluding training and reference computation. CPU is the main timing comparison because recorded wall time contains severe execution interruptions.
