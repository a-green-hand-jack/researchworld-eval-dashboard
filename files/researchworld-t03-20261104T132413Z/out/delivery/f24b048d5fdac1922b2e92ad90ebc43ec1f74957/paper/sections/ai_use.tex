\section*{AI use statement}
In this work, we used generative AI tools for proposing and refining the research questions, designing the methodology and experiments, implementing the methods and analysis code, running the experiments, interpreting the results and analysing the data. The work was carried out by AI agents (Claude models) acting as separate roles of an automated research organisation (context and literature, study plan, feasibility review, analysis, figures and tables, venue formatting, report writing, and internal and external review); the research objective was given to the organisation, and no human author took part in the work itself. We did not use generative AI tools to collect new data; the datasets are public and were downloaded as described in the reproduction notes. Additionally, we used generative AI tools for writing and editing code, drafting and revising the text, searching and reading the literature, formatting references, and creating figures and tables. We have reviewed all AI-assisted work through the checks built into the process: every number in the text is written from the result tables by a script, the experiment pipeline regenerates the tables from raw run files, cited references were looked up in public bibliographic sources, and a role separate from the authoring roles reviewed each delivery against the objective. We take responsibility for the final content of this work, including text, claims or artifacts produced with the aid of generative AI.
