\section{Discussion and limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}
\paragraph{Which method for which condition.}
Marginal validity does not depend on the score, so the choice should rest on region size and conditional coverage, evaluated with the protocol of Table~\ref{tab:protocol}.
For Dirichlet-like noise with covariate-dependent scale, a normalised or likelihood score gives the smallest regions and the best worst-slab coverage; for homoscedastic noise isotropic in log-ratio coordinates the Aitchison distance is best, but it under-covers boundary strata and depends on the zero-replacement constant.
A misspecified mean can reverse these rankings.
For classifier sets, LAC gives the smallest sets and APS or RAPS the better worst size-stratum coverage in two thirds of our synthetic cells, with sizeable exceptions.

\paragraph{What the evaluation results mean for practice.}
A worst-slab coverage from one split is a biased and noisy summary: report the cell size and the size of the bias (Proposition~\ref{prop:min}), use cells of at least about 200 test points, and rank on at least 25 replications with paired differences (Table~\ref{tab:protocol}).
The method-dependent bias reverses pairs of scores up to about $0.2$ apart in true worst-decile coverage with probability $\ge5\%$ in a single evaluation with 500 test points; with 25 averaged replications or $n_{\mathrm{te}}\ge2000$ only pairs closer than about $0.01$--$0.03$.

\paragraph{Limitations and where not to extrapolate.}
\begin{itemize}\setlength\itemsep{0pt}
\item \emph{Synthetic known-truth data.} Two noise families, isotropic in log-ratio coordinates; real compositional data were not studied; classifier sets use one CNN (intervals reflect split variance only).
\item \emph{Mean model and dimension.} Linear clr regression; one misspecification (quadratic, $K=3$) was probed; volume ratios exist for $K\le5$ only.
\item \emph{Other.} CV+ and the jackknife+: two noise models, four scores; counts of ``better'' and ``worse'' paired intervals are descriptive (no multiplicity correction); covariate shift: known density ratio, one dimension; limited literature search (Appendix~\ref{app:repro}).
\end{itemize}
