# Findings per claim (analysis position). Every number is in the cited table; `main_by_rank.csv` rows are `group=all, group_by=rank_mean` (targets not first under the mean); costs are feasible-only medians (`*_med_feas`) unless said; "infeasible" = share of targets for which first place is unreachable within the cap. Sampled-target rank-rule rows are medians over the sample (HELM: all 78 targets; OLLB: 57 distinct targets; OLLB top-500: 116).

**C1 — cost depends on the rule, and the order of rules is not stable across settings.**
HELM (79 models, 57 subjects), cap `ceil`, median L1 (points) / K (tasks): mean 696 / 16, geomean 669 / 16, harmonic 644 / 16, Borda 534 / 47, Condorcet-winner 213 / 29 (= Copeland here), median 112 / 21, min 98 / 11.5, plurality 47 / 9, weighted means 248–289 / 7–9 (`main_by_rank.csv`). Paired L1 ratio to the mean at `ceil`: plurality 0.07, min 0.15, median 0.16, Condorcet 0.30, Borda 0.78, geomean 0.96 (`vs_mean.csv`, both-feasible pairs, bootstrap CIs in the table). On OLLB (4496 models, 6 tasks, ceil) the order differs: Borda is cheapest (12.7 points), then plurality 22.8, Condorcet 23.3, min 43, wmean 63–122, median 106, harmonic 125, geomean 153, mean 181 (L1 / task SD: 0.9 to 12.5). The plurality–mean ratio is 0.07 on HELM and 0.50 on OLLB, and min is 0.15 (HELM) vs 0.25 (OLLB). Kendall tau of the rule order between settings (dataset × cap, feasible-only median L1) has median 0.69 (min 0.19) and for K median 0.45 (range −0.91 to 1) (`rule_order_stability.csv`): the ordering of rules by L1 is moderately stable, by K it is not. K and L1 rank the rules differently (e.g. HELM ceil: Borda has the largest K of all rules, 47, while its L1 is below the mean's; plurality has the smallest L1, 47).
**C2 — mean rule closed form.** L1 = n(τ+ε) − Σx when caps are slack; K = fewest tasks whose headroom covers the need; Linf = need/n. Checked against an LP on 60 real targets per cell, max error 6e-13 (`theory_checks.csv`, T1–T2).
**C3 — median / min and K–n scaling (negative for the worst-case reading).** Upper bounds K ≤ ⌊n/2⌋+1 (median) and K ≤ n (min) hold (T4), but they are worst-case: in the task-subset experiment (HELM, ceil, target at rank 5, 60 draws per n) the median K is 1–3 at n = 3…48 for median and 1–2 for min, far below the bounds (25 and 48 at n = 48); mean K grows 1→4 (`tasksub_summary.csv`). Over all HELM targets at ceil the median K is 21 for the median rule (≈ n/3) and 11.5 for min, not n/2 and n. The claim "median and min force K ≈ n/2 and n" is therefore not supported as a typical-case statement; it is the worst case (a target below the rivals' median/min on every task).
**C4 — caps and feasibility.** At `ceil` and `front0/front2` every target is feasible under every rule on HELM; at `gain10` first place is unreachable for 27–82 % of HELM targets depending on the rule (plurality 0.27, median 0.49, Condorcet/Copeland 0.51, mean 0.63, Borda 0.68, geomean 0.69, min 0.82); at `gain5` 63–91 % (plurality 0.63, min 0.91), at `gain20` 5–56 % (plurality 0.05, median 0.15, mean 0.28, min 0.56), and on OLLB for 25 % (Borda) to 100 % (wmean3/4) with score rules ≥ 0.90 (`main_by_rank.csv`, `infeasible`, `n_feasible`). Costs at tight caps are summarised only over feasible targets and the feasible sets differ by rule, hence the paired ratios with both-feasible counts and `n_only_rule_feasible` / `n_only_mean_feasible` in `vs_mean.csv`. The empirical (`emp95`) cap, derived from OLLB fine-tune-minus-base gains, makes first place unreachable for 93–99 % of OLLB targets under score rules; it is a sensitivity scenario, not a calibration for HELM.
**C5 — rank rules vs score rules.** Plurality and Condorcet-type rules can be far cheaper than the mean on HELM (ratios above) but are less often feasible at tight caps for plurality (OLLB gain10: 81 % infeasible vs Borda 25 %). Cost grows with rank under every rule (log–log slope of L1 on rank: HELM mean 1.13 [0.92, 1.34], median 1.91 [1.71, 2.07], plurality 3.29 [2.21, 4.11]; OLLB mean 0.44, median 0.65, Borda 0.65) (`rank_scaling.csv`). Condorcet is an upper bound for Condorcet-consistent rules; Copeland is never costlier than Condorcet (HELM ceil: equal for 82 % of targets and cheaper for the rest; OLLB top-500 ceil, 27 matched targets with a finished Copeland solve: median L1 8.2 vs 13.7, never costlier). "No Condorcet winner" targets are reported as infeasible for Condorcet; Copeland always has a winner and is the less demanding rank rule. Copeland MILPs hit the 120 s limit for 2 of 30 ceil targets and 2 of 116 at gain10 (reported as `failed`, not infeasible).
**C6 — raw vs normalised scores.** Matched targets, `raw_vs_norm.csv`: median L1(raw)/L1(norm) at ceil is 0.85 (mean), 0.86 (median), 1.61 (min), 1.38 (pow-5), 0.91 (geomean); the rank under the rule of the target is almost never the same (`rank_rule_equal_share` ≈ 0) and K is equal for 29 % (min) to 100 % (Borda, plurality) of targets depending on the rule. Normalisation changes the cost by a rule-dependent factor, up to 1.6× for min; the rule order of costs is not invariant to it.
**C7 — evaluation noise and task bootstrap.** Targets: the 10 models ranked 2–11 by the observed mean (gain10 cap; HELM R = 100 noisy draws, OLLB top-500 R = 50, task bootstrap R = 100). Median over targets of the per-target share of draws with first place unreachable (`noise_summary_*.csv`, `taskboot_summary_helm.csv`): HELM noise 0 for mean, geomean, median, Borda, Condorcet and plurality, 0.28 for min; OLLB top-500 noise 0.60 for min, 0.31 plurality, 0.02 Condorcet, 0 others; HELM task bootstrap 0.18 for min, 0 otherwise. The target's rank under the rule in the noisy draws (`rank_rule_med`) is 6–6.5 for most HELM rules (observed ranks 2–11) but for OLLB min it is ~248 (the min rule ranking is very sensitive to a single low cell). Feasible-only median L1 under noise stays close to the unperturbed order of costs (HELM: mean 174, geomean 170, Borda 50, Condorcet 22, plurality 7.6, median 6.5, min 7.0), so the rule order of C1 survives evaluation noise and task resampling at these targets; the share of draws in which the target is already first is ≤ 0.05 for all rules. Whether a target is "robustly first" after investment at ε = 10⁻³ is not tested: the plan is a cost estimate, not a significance test.
**Caveats.** (i) Rank-rule runs on OLLB are on 57 sampled targets (head-20 + 40 log-uniform ranks); Copeland on the OLLB top-500 slice only for 30 targets at `ceil` and 116 at `gain10` (cost: ~1 h per cap). (ii) `trimmed1` reports L1 only (K not computed). (iii) Costs are in score points of the dataset's own scale; compare datasets only through `L1_over_sd_*`. (iv) HELM has 79 models and 57 subjects, so tail ranks are few; OLLB top-500 and full results differ in scale, so claims are stated per dataset.
