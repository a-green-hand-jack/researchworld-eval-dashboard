Run-reference error and two-run disagreement use the same denominator, max(exact-reference L1,0.01 probability units). Run-reference distances average 30 runs per input; two-run distances average all distinct run pairs once per input, then equally over 60 independent inputs. Supplied pointwise 95% instance-cluster percentile intervals are conditional on the fitted model, split and background. Two-run disagreement is not single-run accuracy; equality of the curves does not validate an explanation. Horizontal positions are mean measured prediction rows per single call. Both methods target the same empirical interventional probability game over grouped raw features with B=32.
