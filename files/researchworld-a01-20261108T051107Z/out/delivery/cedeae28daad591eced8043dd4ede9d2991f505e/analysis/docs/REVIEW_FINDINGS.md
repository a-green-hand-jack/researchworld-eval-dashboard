## Competitor-frame dependence

The5,040-row study holds original-mean positions2–11 fixed, compares the original
frame with removal of its original mean leader and with ten shared random-half
frames, and recomputes all declared rules at ceiling/gain10. The same mean
leader is removed for every rule. The original top500 selection stays fixed;
this study does not reselect the best500 after a removal. Random-half frames
forcibly include the target, so actual rival counts are retained in each row.

For HELM gain10, removing the mean leader lowered optimal K for10/10 Borda
and5/10 mean targets, while minimum K fell for0/10. Under random halves, the
fraction of these ten targets with lower Borda K had median1.0 and empirical
2.5–97.5% range0.1–1.0; for mean it was0.5 [0,1.0]. Thus ordinal costs in
particular depend on the competitor frame. In full OLLB gain10, minimum-rule
feasibility was7/10 originally and median8/10 under random halves, while mean
feasibility stayed10/10. These near-top panels are sensitivity checks, not
summaries over every target. Ten half-frame draws give coarse descriptive
ranges, not a calibrated CI. Score-rule costs cannot increase when rivals are
removed at fixed caps; the checker verifies this property.
