# Restricted literature reading

Only the 25 entries currently listed in `references_provenance.json` are cited. Exact permitted starter IDs and exact foundational-method DOI/title lookups were used. Abstracts were read for relevance; the PMF source PDF was retrieved and its text inspected. No citation networks, related-paper lists, or broad benchmark-prediction searches were used. No prohibited-material encounter was identified during this paper-writing step.

The original supplied bibliography was not used to fill the reference minimum: it largely concerned unrelated recommender applications. A lookup using arXiv 1001.3011 returned an unrelated variance-components title; that record was rejected and the intended spectral-regularization work was resolved by exact title. A title-search result for spectral regularization was also rejected because the returned title did not match. One service supplied a mechanical-device abstract for the EM paper; that content was rejected and the exact DOI was resolved through OpenAlex, whose abstract described incomplete-data likelihood correctly. These were metadata quality failures, not scientific evidence. Old uncited lookup files are not active bibliography entries.

Claims were limited to the consulted content: evaluation breadth, item-versus-benchmark measurement granularity, low-dimensional modeling, matrix-completion assumptions, ridge regularization, prediction-oriented design, missingness, and resampling dependence. No literature results were used as measurements in this study, and no novelty claim against inaccessible or prohibited prior work was made.
