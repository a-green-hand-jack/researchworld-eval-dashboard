# Protocol clarification before main results inspection

The n=30 instance design cannot justify near-certain population reliability merely because all observed runs succeed. Percentile cluster-bootstrap intervals may collapse to [1,1] at that boundary. They will be reported as descriptive intervals, NOT finite-sample certificates. Add a simultaneous one-sided bounded-mean Hoeffding lower bound over the 24 exact-reference dataset/model/budget cells, treating training seeds as fixed and averaging all runs/seeds within each randomly selected instance. Lower = max(0, mean - sqrt(log(24/.05)/(2n))). This conservative bound requires independent instance/stream clusters (or random sampling without replacement from a fixed finite test pool). ACS selects at most one person per household; household-unequal selection is not a population-weighted ACS survey design. No claim of US population representativeness. Because n=30, this bound cannot reach .95 even at observed perfection; report certification inconclusive. The 0.95 criterion remains illustrative.
