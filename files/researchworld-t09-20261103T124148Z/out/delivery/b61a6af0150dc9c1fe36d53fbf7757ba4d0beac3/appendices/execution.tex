\paragraph{Graph construction and fixed inputs.}
Regular graphs use the declared size/degree. Erd\H{o}s--R\'enyi edges have probability $20/(n-1)$. Barab\'asi--Albert graphs attach ten edges per new node, giving realized mean degree near 20. The block model has eight equal blocks, expected degree 20 and mixing $\mu=0.15$: with block size $b=n/8$, within/between probabilities are $20(1-\mu)/(b-1)$ and $20\mu/(n-b)$. Table~\ref{tab:graph-inventory} reports all 36 measured graphs/partitions; these structural attributes match all 102 corrected graph/config metadata records in \path{analysis/v2_dependency_audit.json}. For graph seed $g$, fixed covariates use seed $g+123456$ and BFS partitioning uses $g+97$. Experimental assignment/noise/prediction seeds and master-namespaced pairing identifiers are stored per replicate. Models/mechanisms share streams within a cell; designs/panels use independent replicate streams.

\paragraph{Scientific-definition correction.}
The first full execution was superseded after a final scientific check found that normalized sparse accumulation could put $17/20$ above $0.85$ and $60/80$ below $0.75$. This could misclassify hidden-tail support and threshold outcomes/features despite successful arithmetic audits and selected replay. The corrected one-hop computation counts treated neighbors before dividing by degree, with isolates still using own treatment. It preserves graphs, configurations, seeds, 400 repetitions and all 102 cells; every empirical and exact-risk artifact was regenerated under frozen corrected source. An independent integer-event checker replayed all 40,800 saved assignments using $4k\geq3d$ for the threshold and $20k>17d$ for hidden-tail activation, checking stored maxima/support counts without regression refitting. Its receipt is \path{analysis/boundary_audit.json}. Original outputs/source/receipts remain superseded history in \path{analysis/roundoff_v1/}; pilot boundaries were not repaired and pilots supply calibration only.

\paragraph{Audits and selected clean replay.}
All 102 cells and 306 exact-risk records passed completeness/provenance checks. The internal raw-to-summary audit checked 73,440 scalar and 48,960 paired moments, graph uncertainty and coverage/failure denominators; 280 all-undefined graph/model groups retain missing bounds. Supplementary checks cover 676,164 comparisons and 5,256 independent-stream contrasts. The 13 scientific tests include endpoint targets, conditional error algebra, complement/coupling controls, exact HT enumeration, rational cutoff equality and failure-aware scoring. These are internal validity checks, not independent research review.

The corrected clean replay reran four predeclared cells: regular $n=2000$, degree 4 or 80, seed 201, both assignment designs, all 400 repetitions. Every column and original/replay Parquet hash matched for 115,200 outputs; measured elapsed time was 84.09 seconds. Source/hash receipts are \path{analysis/reproduction_audit.json}. This checks deterministic selected-cell replay, not an independent implementation or full-study reproduction. Source/config/raw/aggregate hashes and the complete failure ledger are preserved separately.

\paragraph{Measured compute and its limits.}

These definitions differ from per-function sums and include/exclude different overheads; they must not be conflated into one wall-time estimate. Maximum recorded simulation/exact-risk process RSS was 496,880 KiB; the later summary/audit stage recorded cumulative child-process peak RSS 522,460 KiB. Neither is a container-wide peak measurement.
