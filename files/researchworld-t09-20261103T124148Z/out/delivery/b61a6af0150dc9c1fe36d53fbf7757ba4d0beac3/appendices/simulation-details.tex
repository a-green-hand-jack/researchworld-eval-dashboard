\section{Additional response and fitting details}
\label{app:simulation-details}
The normalized sigmoid response is
\[
 h_i=\frac{\operatorname{expit}(12(s_i-0.5))-\operatorname{expit}(-6)}{\operatorname{expit}(6)-\operatorname{expit}(-6)}.
\]
All non-DIM regressions contain an intercept, own treatment and the fixed covariate. The quadratic/cubic models include all exposure powers through their degree. The two-hop model adds $s$ and $t$. The spline adds a cubic B-spline basis with five equally spaced knots from zero to one, no bias basis column and linear extrapolation; its basis and knots are fixed before outcomes. The threshold oracle adds the true $\mathbf1\{s\geq0.75\}$ feature. Singular values are retained above machine precision times the larger design-matrix dimension times its largest singular value. Endpoint contrasts use the same feature pipeline at both global assignments. HC3 clips $1-h_{ii}$ below $10^{-10}$ for numerical stability; this does not repair misspecification or correlated errors. Complete estimator/source definitions and denominator policies are supplied in \path{analysis/methods.md} and \path{code/src/gatebench/core.py}.
