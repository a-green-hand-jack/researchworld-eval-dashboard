All quantities below are generated by `diagnostics/write_evidence_note.py` from the saved result files; error units are percentage points. This is the analysis owner's evidence handoff, not a manuscript or a novelty claim.

There are1,611,440 immutable target predictions in22,360 benchmark/scenario/budget/draw/method cells. Every access/cell check and independent truth/baseline/clipping/error check passed. The crossfit candidate improves macro score error over random means and fixed source-difference, but has larger MAE/RMSE than learned-score ridge at every studied budget. The finite-population expectation identity does not establish predictive-risk or ranking superiority. These are finite-set observations; no universal method winner, future-model coverage or new residual-correction novelty follows.

## Numerical validity, chronology and failures

Raw exact-float rank processing fragmented mathematical ties. The initial outputs are preserved in `original_raw_ties/`. The post-scoring repair is explicitly dated in `notes/postscore_amendments/2026-11-05_rank_numerics.json`; it changes no predictions, fitting, tuning or target access. All80,840 error/bias/tail/out-of-range rows remain bitwise identical, all1,420 constant-shift rank/tie/inversion comparisons pass, and22,360 independent12-decimal rank calculations agree (`rank_correction_checks.json`). Precision10 differs from12 in one cell (maximum Spearman0.000141/tau-b0.000160); precision14 changes27 cells, with maximum Spearman0.098376 (6 source-difference cells,20 copied MuSR anchor cells from one fixed fit, and1 learned-imputer cell). The independently verified count-rank ties explain the first26; rounding does not prove that every nearly equal learned prediction is a mathematical tie. Twelve decimals is one fixed operational numerical tie convention, not a rule selected per method/dataset and not a universal translation-invariance theorem. Retain that sensitivity rather than asserting complete numerical identity. `rank_precision_macro_contrasts.csv` and `rank_precision_conclusion_sensitivity.csv` quantify the resulting primary macro contrast/endpoint changes. The1pp pair-gap boundary is canonicalized as part of this numerical fix. No ranking finding was used to refit/select methods.

Original v1 authorization, exact-hash v2 extension and target execution chronology are preserved in `config/gate.json`, `gate_followup.json` and `notes/execution_gate.md`. Formal amended-scorer-hash GO publication followed prediction launch; it is not backdated. The plan owner states no rerun is needed because predictor/tuning/data/split/item bytes are unchanged. Initial whole-matrix audit access is disclosed separately from the enforced predictor API budgets; this is a reproducible guard, not a cryptographic holdout. One supplemental formula-diagnostic job failed because a glob included timing parquet files; it was fixed and rerun with no primary result dropped (`notes/run_failures.json`). Numerical raw-rank repair and initially capped fits are not concealed.
