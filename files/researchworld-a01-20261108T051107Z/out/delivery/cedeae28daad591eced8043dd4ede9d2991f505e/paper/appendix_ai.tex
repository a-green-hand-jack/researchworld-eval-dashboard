\section{AI use statement}
\label{app:ai}
This study, including its research design, mathematical statements and proofs, experiment design, implementation, data cleaning, execution of experiments, interpretation of results, figures, literature search and the writing of this manuscript, was carried out by AI language-model agents working as separate roles (planning, analysis, presentation, venue, report writing and independent review) in an automated research organisation. No human wrote the content. All hypotheses, the investment model, the aggregation rules and the claims were proposed by the agents. Verification consisted of unit tests of the solvers against brute-force search and independent LP/convex oracles, an automated invariant checker on the full result files, independent review by a separate agent role, and, for the completed runs, recomputation of summaries and figures from the stored result files by the scripts in the supplement, and a clean recomputation of every result file from the pinned processed matrices (Appendix~\ref{app:repro}). Only references whose abstract or text an agent actually consulted are cited. Agents may nevertheless have made errors that these checks did not catch.
