## Why the revised planning model is reasonable, and what it assumes

The retained slope rho=1.860141102946608 and A=max(0,elapsed-rho*CPU)=57.6765442939s form an empirical envelope over that complete eligible timing population. They separate a bounded observed additive excess from work proportional to CPU; a very early positive elapsed cost divided by very little CPU otherwise becomes a huge ratio applied to every future CPU second. This is a reasonable prospective alternative given the actual recorded early-prefix behavior. It does not identify the cause, prove overhead occurs only once, or imply the same envelope will contain future runs.

For every unfinished configuration the proposal charges a full repriced CPU run, even for the interrupted configuration, plus the largest observed excess A; it retains four interpreter startups and applies1.5 to both CPU and additive terms:

`reserved wall = 1.5 * [rho*(full unfinished CPU + startup CPU) + N*A]`.
